Eladria

What was once a world of light and color has now succumbed to the Dusk and the grey.

Long ago, humans and kehars shared Eladria in uneasy peace, until a handful of kehars reached for power the world couldn't give them and something answered. The krens came. What began as gifts became corruption, and for a hundred years remembered as the Era of Sorrow, Eladria belonged to them: people enslaved, hunted, sacrificed, while kehars fled to the fringes and hid.

Then the Allmother rose out of the last of humanity's hope and drove the krens back far enough to raise Dawnkeep, the first Free City. Her own First Followers murdered her for more power than she'd give them, an act so vile it left them wandering shells of themselves, still out there somewhere. In her place, the Dusk came.

The Dusk

The Dusk isn't simple darkness. It's corruption that spreads through reality itself. Wherever it reaches, color drains out, the land withers, and magic turns unpredictable; a spell might come out stronger, weaker, or into something its caster never meant.

Stay in it too long and it changes you if it doesn't kill you first. Plants grow strange and enormous, animals turn monstrous, people lose pieces of their mind, or themselves. The ruined land past the Beacons is called the Dusklands. People do live out there, protected by magic and sacrifice, alongside the dusktouched the corruption has made and the duskborn horrors the Dusk gave rise to on its own. Nobody really knows what the Dusk is, or why it came the moment the Allmother died.

Where You Live

The center of civilization calls itself Mendris. Dawnkeep, the last great capital, and the Frontiers, the fortified farmsteads that keep it fed. Dawnkeep was raised by the Allmother herself, and the towering Beacons shining with her golden light are the only reason the city, or anything else in Mendris, is still unaffected by the Dusk.

Survival came at a cost, however. Where you're born mostly decides how you live; cityfolk stay cityfolk, farmsteaders stay farmsteaders, and inside Dawnkeep your Sector shapes your work, your standing, everyone you're likely to ever meet. Travel is tightly controlled not just for order, but because almost nowhere past the walls is safe to wander into.

Who You Might Be

Humans are found everywhere in Mendris, with no single gift so much as a talent for making do, building communities, and surviving circumstances that should have ended them, again and again.

Kehars are said to be born from dreams; a rare few can shape their dreams into reality. In a world losing its color, kehars keep theirs ye it makes them easy to spot, and for some in Mendris, easy to distrust for what their ancestors once did.

Shanats descend from humans and kehars touched by kren blood. They inheritted strange abilities tied to darkness, small physical peculiarities, and, in the Shantari family, one of the Great Houses that rule Mendris, that kren connection gave them the old codes that let them read and operate kren factories nobody else understands. While powerful, the touch of the kren makes any use of their powers prohibitted in Mendris, so that means that shanats usually use them in secret.

The Table's Tone

Eladria is bleak, but Dawnkeep's whole existence is proof that bleak isn't the same as hopeless. People have dreams and hopes and the Dusk has been a constant for everyones life, making it a threat to avoid that a reminder of the life before. For most people, life before the Dusk is but a fairytale. Expect the Dusk to show up as body horror at the edges, prejudice to be a real and sometimes ugly social force, and no easy answers about who gets protected and who doesn't.

If any of that isn't for you, or you just want to know where the table's lines are, ask before we start. That's what session zero is for.

Quick reference

Beacon
Kren technology fused with the Allmother's Light and the only thing standing between a settlement and the Dusk.

An invisible corruption that drains color, twists magic, and slowly unmakes what it touches.
Dusklands
The ruined world beyond the Beacons' reach.
Dusktouched / Duskborn
What the Dusk changes, and what it makes outright.
Kren
The beings that answered the old pleas for power, and never left.
Kehar
Born from dreams; still vividly colorful in a greying world.
Shanat
Human-and-kehar descendants with kren blood — strange gifts, stranger secrets.
Sector
The Dawnkeep neighborhood you're born into, and most of what it decides about your life.

The fortified farmsteads that feed Dawnkeep, and the first line against the dark.
