ConditionsStrain and Bouts of Madness

A condition doesn't stack unless it says so (Exhausted does). Gaining a condition you already have keeps whichever duration is longer.

BleedingBlindedDeafenedDeterminedDisorientedExhaustedFrightenedGrappledImmobilizedInspiredObscuredProneSlowedStunnedSurprisedUnconsciousWounded

Bleeding

Condition

Persistent damage: 1 lethal damage at the end of each of your turns, until the duration runs out or the wound is treated (with First aid, Field Medicine or Avoid Death).

Blinded

Condition

Rolls that rely on sight use a chance die. Attacking or acting against a target you can hear, such as an adjacent enemy, takes −3d instead, as in darkness. You can't Dodge, and your other defense rolls take −2d.

Deafened

Condition

Rolls that rely on hearing use a chance die, and you take −1d to notice anything around you. You can't benefit from anything that needs you to hear someone, such as a shouted order or a command, unless you can see them.

Determined

Condition

Your next Stress cost of 2 or more is reduced by 1. A cost of 1 can't be reduced, so it doesn't use up Determined.

Disoriented

Condition

You can't use reactions.

Exhausted

Condition

−1d to all rolls. Exhausted can stack, giving −1d for each stack. When your stacks reach your Wounded threshold (Stamina + Resolve), you collapse: you fall Unconscious until your stacks drop below it, usually at the end of a rest. If you gain another stack while collapsed, you die.

Frightened

Condition

−1d to all rolls, or −2d instead on rolls against the source of your fear. Moving closer to the source of your fear costs 1 Stress each time you choose to do it.

Grappled

Condition

You can't Move, Dodge or Disengage, and you can only attack unarmed, with a Light weapon, or with the Grapple action. Ends when someone breaks free.

Immobilized

Condition

You can't move.

Inspired

Condition

After a failed roll, reroll every die that didn't succeed. Inspired ends when used.

Obscured

Condition

Rolls to see or target you take −1d, and you can try to hide with Stealth. Smoke, fog, shadow and some powers cause it.

Prone

Condition

You're on the ground: melee attacks against you get +1d, ranged attacks −1d (see Position). Standing up takes 1 action.

Slowed

Condition

Your Speed is halved.

Stunned

Condition

In combat, you lose any reactions, and on your turn you gain two fewer actions and don't gain a reaction. Out of combat, you're overwhelmed: you move and react more slowly to your situation, at the GM's discretion.

Surprised

Condition

You must take a slow turn and can't react.

Unconscious

Condition

You can't act or react. If damage knocked you out, see Avoiding death and Waking up.

Wounded

Condition

−1d to all rolls if the first box past your threshold holds bashing, −2d if lethal, −3d if aggravated. Ends when your total damage drops to your Wounded threshold or below.

Strain and Bouts of Madness

Tenacity check: each incident that deals Tenacity damage calls for one check, however many points it cost: Endure, rolling one die per point of Tenacity you have left (after the damage), and needing successes equal to the Tenacity damage from the incident. The more of your mind is gone, the less of it holds on. On a success, you hold together. On a failure, you suffer Strain if the damage came from your own Stress overflowing (pushing yourself, abilities, extra reactions, staying up, running a pool empty), or a Bout of Madness if it came from anything else (horror, the supernatural, a setting's threats). If an incident had both causes, it's a Bout of Madness.

Rolling Strain or a Bout: roll 3d6, add the dice together, and subtract the total Tenacity you're missing. Some Scars add a further penalty. Look up the result on its table below.

Strain

Your body and nerves give out. Strain never decides what you do: it gives conditions and penalties.

ResultEffect
0 or lessHeart gives out. You die.
1Collapse. You fall Unconscious. At the end of each minute, roll 1d; a success ends it and you come to.
2Blackout. You become Stunned. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
3Retching. You're Disoriented and take −1d to all rolls until it ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
4Shaking. You take −2d to Physical rolls until it ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
5–6Tunnel vision. You're Disoriented until it ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
7–8Rattled. You're Frightened of the nearest threat until it ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
9–11Winded. You're Slowed and take −1d to all rolls until it ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
12–14Adrenaline. You get +1d to Physical rolls until it ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends it.
15–16Steady. You get +1d to all rolls until the end of the next round.
17 or moreSecond wind. You regain the Tenacity lost in this incident.

Bouts of Madness

The mind breaks under horror. A Bout of Madness can take your choices away.

ResultEffect
0 or lessDeath. Your heart stops and you die.
1Catatonia. You fall Prone and can't act or react. At the end of each hour, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
2Stricken. You become Stunned. At the end of each minute, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
3Sickened. You vomit uncontrollably and are Disoriented until the madness ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
4Hallucinations. You believe vermin infest your body. Until the madness ends, you must use an action each turn to cut or claw yourself, unless you're prevented. Each time, you take 1 bashing damage. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
5–6Violence. Hate overwhelms you. Until the madness ends, you must take a fast turn each round and use an action to attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
7–8Panic. Until the madness ends, you must use an action each turn to rush away from the source of the madness, and you can't willingly move closer to it. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
9–11Confusion. You are Disoriented until the madness ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
12–14Rage. Until the madness ends, you get +1d to weapon attacks. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
15–16Resolute. You get +1d to all rolls until the end of the next round, when the madness ends.
17 or moreRevelation. You regain the Tenacity lost in this incident. From now on, you get +1 to all Strain and Bout of Madness rolls.
