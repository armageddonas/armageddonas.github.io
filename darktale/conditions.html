A condition doesn't stack unless it says so (Exhausted does). Gaining a condition you already have keeps whichever duration is longer.

Beaten Down

Condition

Making an attack costs 1 Stress, unless you have no chance to surrender (the enemy won't or can't accept it, such as a mindless beast). If you surrender, clear 1 Stress. Ends when your total damage drops to your Beaten Down threshold or below.

Bleeding

Condition

Persistent damage: 1 lethal damage at the end of each of your turns, until the duration runs out or the wound is treated (with First aid, Field Medicine or Avoid Death).

Blinded

Condition

Rolls that rely on sight use a chance die. Attacking or acting against a target you can hear, such as an adjacent enemy, takes −3d instead, as in darkness. You can't Dodge, and your other defense rolls take −2d.

Deafened

Condition

Rolls that rely on hearing use a chance die, and you take −1d to notice anything around you. You can't benefit from anything that needs you to hear someone, such as a shouted order or a command, unless you can see them.

Determined

Condition

Your next Stress cost is reduced by 1.

Disoriented

Condition

You can't use reactions.

Exhausted

Condition

−1d to all rolls. Exhausted can stack, giving −1d for each stack. When your stacks reach your Beaten Down threshold (Stamina + Resolve), you collapse: you fall Unconscious until your stacks drop below it, usually at the end of a rest. If you gain another stack while collapsed, you die.

Frightened

Condition

−1d to all rolls, or −2d instead on rolls against the source of your fear. Moving closer to the source of your fear costs 1 Stress each time you choose to do it.

Grappled

Condition

You can't Move, Dodge or Disengage, and you can only attack unarmed, with a Light weapon, or with the Grapple action. Ends when someone breaks free.

Immobilized

Condition

You can't move.

Inspired

Condition

After a failed roll, reroll every die that didn't succeed. Inspired ends when used.

Obscured

Condition

Rolls to see or target you take −1d, and you can try to hide with Stealth. Smoke, fog, shadow and some powers cause it.

Prone

Condition

You're on the ground: melee attacks against you get +1d, ranged attacks −1d (see Position). Standing up takes 1 action.

Slowed

Condition

Your Speed is halved.

Stunned

Condition

In combat, you lose any reactions, and on your turn you gain two fewer actions and don't gain a reaction. Out of combat, you're overwhelmed: you move and react more slowly to your situation, at the GM's discretion.

Surprised

Condition

You must take a slow turn and can't react.

Unconscious

Condition

You can't act or react. If damage knocked you out, see Avoiding death and Waking up.

Bouts of Madness

Each incident that deals Tenacity damage triggers one Bout of Madness roll, however many points it cost. Roll 3d6, add the dice together, and subtract the total Tenacity you're missing. Some Scars add a further penalty. Look up the result below.

ResultEffect
0 or lessDeath. Your heart stops and you die.
1Catatonia. You fall Prone and can't act or react. At the end of each hour, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
2Stricken. You become Stunned. At the end of each minute, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
3Sickened. You vomit uncontrollably and are Disoriented until the madness ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
4Hallucinations. You believe vermin infest your body. Until the madness ends, you must use an action each turn to cut or claw yourself, unless you're prevented. Each time, you take 1 bashing damage. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
5–6Violence. Hate overwhelms you. Until the madness ends, you must take a fast turn each round and use an action to attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
7–8Panic. Until the madness ends, you must use an action each turn to rush away from the source of the madness, and you can't willingly move closer to it. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
9–11Confusion. You are Disoriented until the madness ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
12–14Rage. Until the madness ends, you get +1d to weapon attacks. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
15–16Resolute. You get +1d to all rolls until the end of the next round, when the madness ends.
17 or moreRevelation. You regain the Tenacity lost in this incident. From now on, you get +1 to all Bout of Madness rolls.
