Armor, weapons and kits

Armor

  • Armor rating: When an attack hits or grazes you, subtract your armor rating from the damage. Damage that gets through keeps its type. If armor absorbs all of it, you still take 1 bashing damage.
  • Wear: Armor has a Wear track with boxes equal to its current rating. It takes 1 Wear when a hit gets past it (damage beyond the 1-bashing floor) or when the attack scores an Opportunity, at most 1 Wear per hit. You can also have your armor take 1 Wear to ignore one condition a hit would cause, such as Prone, Bleeding or Stunned, or being pushed back.
  • Battered: When the Wear track fills, the armor's rating drops by 1. A full Wear track takes no more Wear from hits. You can still choose to have it take Wear to ignore a condition; each point that doesn't fit lowers the rating by 1 more. Prepare at a rest clears Wear. If a rest ends with the Wear track still full, the rating drops by 1 more. Lost rating comes back only when the armor is repaired during downtime.
  • Broken: Armor reduced to rating 0 is broken. It gives no protection until repaired during downtime.

Weapon traits

  • Deadly: An Opportunity on the attack adds +1 lethal damage.
  • Discreet: When someone searches you, finding this item takes 1 more success than finding anything else (see Searching someone).
  • Heavy: Needs Strength 3; −1d per missing dot.
  • Knockdown: On an Opportunity, the target falls Prone.
  • Light: Small and one-handed. Usable while Grappled.
  • Piercing X: Ignores X points of armor rating.
  • Precise: On called shots, 6s explode.
  • Quickdraw: Drawing or stowing it is a free action.
  • Range: Attacks beyond it take −2d, up to double the range.
  • Reach: Attack a target up to 2 meters away.
  • Shield (X): +X dice to Parry while held. With Shield (2) or higher, it also counts as cover against ranged attacks.
  • Slow: Reloading takes 1 action.
  • Stunning: On an Opportunity, the target is Stunned until the end of their next turn.
  • Throwable: Can be thrown with Dexterity + Ranged, Range 10 m.
  • Two-handed: Needs both hands, so you can't hold a shield, a second weapon or a light source while attacking with it.
  • Versatile: Can be used one- or two-handed; +1 damage when used two-handed.

Kits

A kit is a set of tools for one kind of work. It gives +1d to the rolls it covers. Working with no proper tools at all gives −1d.

  • Charges: a kit has 3 charges. Spend 1 each time you take its +1d. A Complication on that roll spends 1 more.
  • Empty kit: at 0 charges you still have the tools (no −1d), but you get no bonus.
  • Refilling: each Prepare at a rest refills 1 charge on each of your kits. In a settlement you can buy extra rest actions (see Rest) to Prepare again. A week of downtime refills every kit to full.

Other consumables (ammunition, lamp oil, rations, bandages beyond a kit) aren't counted. If you've used Prepare, you have them; a Complication can still use them up.

Encumbrance

Every item has a bulk from 1 to 3, or no bulk at all. Add up the bulk of everything you carry, including worn armor, to find your load.

BulkExamples
NonePapers, money in handfuls, clothing, a knife, a sap and other Discreet items
1One-handed weapons, a pistol, a small shield, each kit, a lantern, light armor, a bag of wealth
2Two-handed weapons, one-handed heavy weapons, bows, crossbows, rifles, a large shield, medium armor
3Two-handed heavy weapons, heavy armor, a chest of wealth

The weapon and armor tables list each item's bulk.

Load limits depend on Strength: a light load is up to Strength + 1, a normal load up to twice that, and a heavy load up to three times that.

  • Light load: +1d to Athletics and Stealth.
