Core Rules · Chapter XI

Conditions

Conditions have no built-in duration. The cause sets how long they last.

In Duskworld
PrimerOverviewRunetech

A condition doesn't stack unless it says so (Exhausted does). Gaining a condition you already have keeps whichever duration is longer.

ConditionEffect
Beaten DownMaking an attack costs 1 Stress, unless you have no chance to surrender (the enemy won't or can't accept it, such as a mindless beast). If you surrender, clear 1 Stress. Ends when your total damage drops to your Beaten Down threshold or below.
BleedingPersistent damage: 1 lethal damage at the end of each of your turns, until the duration runs out or the wound is treated (with First aid, Field Medicine or Avoid Death).
BlindedRolls that rely on sight use a chance die. Attacking or acting against a target you can hear, such as an adjacent enemy, takes −3d instead, as in darkness. You can't Dodge, and your other defense rolls take −2d.
DeafenedRolls that rely on hearing use a chance die, and you take −1d to notice anything around you. You can't benefit from anything that needs you to hear someone, such as a shouted order or a command, unless you can see them.
DeterminedYour next Stress cost is reduced by 1.
DisorientedYou can't use reactions.
Exhausted−1d to all rolls. Exhausted can stack, giving −1d for each stack. When your stacks reach your Beaten Down threshold (Stamina + Resolve), you collapse: you fall Unconscious until your stacks drop below it, usually at the end of a rest. If you gain another stack while collapsed, you die.
Frightened−1d to all rolls, or −2d instead on rolls against the source of your fear. Moving closer to the source of your fear costs 1 Stress each time you choose to do it.
GrappledYou can't Move, Dodge or Disengage, and you can only attack unarmed, with a Light weapon, or with the Grapple action. Ends when someone breaks free.
ImmobilizedYou can't move.
InspiredAfter a failed roll, reroll every die that didn't succeed. Inspired ends when used.
ObscuredRolls to see or target you take −1d, and you can try to hide with Stealth. Smoke, fog, shadow and some powers cause it.
ProneYou're on the ground: melee attacks against you get +1d, ranged attacks −1d (see Position). Standing up takes 1 action.
SlowedYour Speed is halved.
StunnedIn combat, you lose any reactions, and on your turn you gain two fewer actions and don't gain a reaction. Out of combat, you're overwhelmed: you move and react more slowly to your situation, at the GM's discretion.
SurprisedYou must take a slow turn and can't react.
UnconsciousYou can't act or react. If damage knocked you out, see Avoiding death and Waking up.
