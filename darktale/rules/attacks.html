Attacks

Every attack is contested, and ties go to the side that acts.

AttacksGrappling
  • Attack pools: Strength + Weaponry for melee weapons, Strength + Brawl for unarmed attacks, and Dexterity + Ranged for ranged and thrown weapons. Talents can change the attribute for some weapons.
  • Hits: Every attack is contested (see Defense and reactions). It hits if the attacker's successes equal or beat the defender's; a tie is a hit with 0 net successes, dealing the weapon's damage alone.
  • Damage: A hit deals damage equal to the net successes plus the weapon's damage, of the weapon's damage type. Armor reduces it (see Armor).
  • Opportunity on attacks is based on the attacker's total successes, not net successes.
  • Off-hand attack: while holding a Light weapon in your other hand, take 1 Stress to make one extra attack with it (1 action). This is an exception to the one-attack rule.
  • Extra attacks: each attack after your first in a turn takes a cumulative −2d (−2d on the second, −4d on the third), whatever grants it. A single action that attacks several targets, such as Unstoppable Charge, counts as one attack.

Called shots

Before rolling an attack, you can aim at a specific spot. Take the penalty, and on a hit, add the effect:

TargetPenaltyEffect on a hit
Arm or hand−1dThe target drops what it's holding
Leg−1dThe target is Slowed
Head−2d+1 damage
Gap in armor−3dIgnore the target's armor rating; the 1-bashing floor still applies

Grappling

  • Grapple 1 action, counts as your attack start or act in a grapple.
  • Starting a grapple: Roll Strength + Brawl, contested like any attack (Dodge, Parry or Defense). On a hit, you and the target are both Grappled. It deals no damage.
  • Acting in a grapple: While Grappled, roll Strength + Brawl, contested by the opponent's Strength + Brawl. On a success, choose one move, or two on an Opportunity:
    • Break free: you're no longer Grappled.
    • Damage: deal bashing damage equal to your net successes, or lethal damage equal to your net successes + weapon damage if you control a Light weapon.
    • Control weapon: take control of a weapon in the grapple, or draw your own Light weapon.
    • Disarm: a weapon you control drops or is thrown away.
    • Drop Prone: you both fall Prone. You can instead drop only the opponent and break free.
    • Hold: until your next turn, the opponent can only take the Grapple action, to break free.
    • Restrain: with rope, cuffs or a hold that locks, the opponent is Immobilized even after you let go. Requires Hold first.
    • Take cover: until your next turn, ranged attacks against you hit the opponent instead.
