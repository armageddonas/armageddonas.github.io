NPCs and companions

One rating from 1 to 5, rolled double for what they do best.

NPCs

  • Each NPC has a single rating from 1 to 5. They roll double the rating for things they're proficient in, the rating as-is for things they know, and a chance die for everything else. Enemy types can add modifiers.
  • When a talent says a roll is "contested by" a pool (such as Intelligence + Resolve), an NPC rolls their rating by the rule above instead.
  • Stress: Minions have none. Important NPCs have Stress equal to twice their rating. Bosses have four times their rating.
  • No Tenacity: NPCs have no Tenacity track, so their Stress doesn't overflow. An NPC with a full Stress track can't pay Stress costs.
  • Minions count as having a full Stress track, so they can't pay Stress costs.
  • Health: Important NPCs have Health equal to Size + rating. Bosses have custom Health, usually double an important NPC's. Minions have no Health track (below).
  • Wounded: Important NPCs and bosses are Wounded when their total damage exceeds half their Health, rounded up.
  • Taking out minions: Any lethal or aggravated damage takes a minion out: they're down and bleeding to death. Bashing damage takes a minion out only if a single hit deals bashing at least equal to its rating; smaller hits are shrugged off. A minion taken out by bashing is unconscious, surrenders or flees, whichever fits the scene.
  • Defense: Minions roll their rating to defend against attacks; they don't Dodge or Parry. Important NPCs and bosses have a Defense set by their type and use the same rules as PCs.
  • Armor: A minion's armor is a static rating with no Wear track. Important NPCs and bosses track Wear like PCs.
  • Damage: NPC attacks deal damage like PCs': net successes plus weapon damage.
  • Boss turns: a boss takes both a fast turn and a slow turn each round (2 actions, then 3), with one attack in each. Its reaction refreshes at the start of each of its turns, as for any character. Once per round, right after a PC's turn, it can take 2 Stress for 1 action that isn't an attack. On its turn, it can take 1 Stress to end one condition on itself. Conditions apply to each of its turns as usual: Stunned costs it 2 actions on every turn it lasts, and a Surprised boss takes only its slow turn.

Companions

A companion is an animal that works with you. It's an NPC with a rating (1–5) and rolls like one: double its rating for what it's proficient in, its rating for what it knows, and a chance die for everything else.

  • Health: Size + rating. It heals like a character; Recover on an animal uses Animal Ken instead of Medicine.
  • No Stress: it counts as having a full Stress track, so it can't pay Stress costs.
  • Defense: it rolls its rating to defend, like a minion. It doesn't Dodge or Parry.
  • Acting: it has no turn of its own. On your turn, you can spend your own actions to have it act. Talents such as Animal Bond give it actions for free.
  • Orders: outside a fight, unusual or dangerous orders need an Animal Ken roll.
  • Getting one: Animal Bond gives a rating 2 animal of your choice: one from the table, or another animal you and the GM build at rating 2. Otherwise, a trained animal costs a bag (a wolfhound, 2 bags).
