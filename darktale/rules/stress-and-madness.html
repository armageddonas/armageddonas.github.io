Stress

  • Stress track: Your current Tenacity + 2 boxes (see Tenacity and madness). As you take Tenacity damage, your Stress track shrinks.
  • Shrinking track: If your Stress track shrinks below the Stress you already have, the Stress that no longer fits is lost. It doesn't overflow again.
  • Push yourself: Before a roll, take 2 Stress to add +2d to it.
  • Extra reaction: Once your reaction for the round is spent, take 2 Stress to react again. You can repeat this as long as you can pay.
  • Recovery roll: Resolve + Composure. Clear 1 Stress per success.
  • Clearing Stress: Stress doesn't clear on its own with time. It clears at a rest (see Rest), at the end of a week of downtime, by catching your breath (see Actions), through talents, by surrendering while Beaten Down, and when it overflows (below).
  • Overflow: You can still take Stress when your track is full, whether you chose to or an effect forced it on you. When Stress doesn't fit, it overflows:
    1. Each point that doesn't fit becomes 1 Tenacity damage. Your Stress track shrinks accordingly, and stays full.
    2. You make one madness check for the incident, and on a failure suffer a Bout of Madness (see Tenacity and madness).
    3. Unless a Bout killed you, clear half your Stress, rounded up. Any madness still plays out.

Only overflow clears Stress this way. Tenacity damage from other sources calls for a madness check too, but clears no Stress.

Tenacity and madness

  • Tenacity measures a character's sanity. Your Tenacity track has Resolve + Composure + 3 boxes.
  • Tenacity damage: Comes from Stress overflow and from effects that say so.
  • Madness check: each incident that deals Tenacity damage calls for one check, however many points it cost: Endure against madness, rolling one die per point of Tenacity you have left (after the damage), and needing successes equal to the Tenacity damage from the incident. The more of your mind is gone, the less of it holds on. On a success, you hold together, and there's no Bout. On a failure, you suffer a Bout of Madness.
  • Bout of Madness: roll 3d6, add the dice together, and subtract the total Tenacity you're missing. Some Scars add a further penalty. Look up the result below.
  • Recovery: Tenacity doesn't heal on its own. It heals only through the Recuperate downtime action (see Downtime).
ResultEffect
0 or lessDeath. Your heart stops and you die.
1Catatonia. You fall Prone and can't act or react. At the end of each hour, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
2Stricken. You become Stunned. At the end of each minute, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.

5–6Violence. Hate overwhelms you. Until the madness ends, you must take a fast turn each round and use an action to attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.

9–11Confusion. You are Disoriented until the madness ends. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
12–14Rage. Until the madness ends, you get +1d to weapon attacks. At the end of each round, roll 1d; a success ends the madness.
15–16Resolute. You get +1d to all rolls until the end of the next round, when the madness ends.
17 or moreRevelation. You regain the Tenacity lost in this incident. From now on, you get +1 to all Bout of Madness rolls.

Taken by the Dusk

A character can be lost to the Dusk in two ways:

  • Taken by the Dusk: a character with Dusking 1 or more who dies on a Bout of Madness. In Mendris, any death that leaves no sign of physical harm, while the victim had at least Dusking 1, is said to be taken by the Dusk.
  • Dusktouched: a character whose Dusking reaches their Resolve + Composure without dying first. They don't die. They change.

Because Dusking only rises with Tenacity damage from the Dusk, missing Tenacity and Dusking tend to rise together during a trip, so the Bout penalty effectively counts twice. Chance of death when a failed madness check brings a Bout:

Missing Tenacity + DuskingDeath chance
1 + 10%
2 + 22%
3 + 39%
4 + 426%

Tenacity heals in downtime; Dusking doesn't. A character who recovers between trips still carries their Dusking into every Bout.
