Damage and Health

Bashing, lethal and aggravated, and the marks that never heal.

  • Health track: Size + Stamina boxes.
  • Damage types: From least to most severe: bashing, lethal and aggravated.
  • Filling the track: The track stays sorted by severity: aggravated damage fills from the left, then lethal, then bashing. New damage that is more severe pushes less severe damage to the right.
  • Overflow: When your Health track is full, a new wound replaces your least severe wound if that one is lighter (lethal on a track with bashing replaces a bashing). If none is lighter, the new wound worsens a step at a time (bashing becomes lethal, lethal becomes aggravated) until one is, and replaces it. Even bashing can turn a lethal wound aggravated this way.
  • Last box:
    • Bashing in your last Health box: you fall Unconscious.
    • Lethal in your last Health box: you fall Unconscious and start Bleeding.
    • Aggravated can only reach your last box once the whole track is aggravated: you die.
  • Staying up: When damage would knock you Unconscious, you can push yourself to stay on your feet: take 2 Stress (it can overflow as usual) and you don't fall. Staying up doesn't heal you: the damage stays in your last box (lethal still makes you Bleed), and each further point of damage would knock you out again, unless you pay again.
  • Waking up: Once you're Unconscious from damage, you wake when your last Health box clears, whether through natural healing or an ally's healing. An ally can also shake, slap or shout you awake with the Wake up action (2 actions, adjacent, no roll; 1 action with smelling salts). Waking this way heals nothing: the damage stays in your last box (lethal still makes you Bleed), and one more point of damage knocks you out again. It doesn't work on a character who chose to Avoid Death, or who collapsed from Exhausted.
  • Wounded threshold: Stamina + Resolve. When your total damage, of any type, exceeds it, you are Wounded: −1d to all rolls if the first box past your threshold holds bashing, −2d if lethal, −3d if aggravated. The track is sorted worst first, so the penalty grows as the wounds past your threshold get worse.

Avoiding death

When damage knocks you Unconscious, you choose what happens next.

  • Avoid Death: take a Death's Mark. You stop Bleeding, and further damage can't kill you while you're unconscious. You can't be targeted by attacks unless an opponent specifically targets unconscious creatures (a creature's description says so). You wake only when an ally heals 1 or more of your damage, or when the party finishes a rest. You've chosen to step out of the fight, and the only other way to act is a Blaze of Glory.
  • If you don't avoid death, the normal rules apply: you may keep Bleeding, and further damage can kill you (see Waking up).
  • Blaze of Glory: each time your turn comes while you're still unconscious, whether or not you avoided death, you can choose to go out in a Blaze of Glory. Take one turn as normal (fast or slow). Each roll you make counts as already having one success with Opportunity. At the end of the turn, you cross through the veil of death.

Scars

A Scar is a lasting mark on a character. Scars don't heal with rest or downtime; only the story can remove one.

Some Scars build up in stages and are written with their stage, Name N: a Scar called Corruption at its second stage is Corruption 2. Settings define these, including what each stage does and what happens at the last one. They're separate from Death's Marks and don't count toward their limit.

In Duskworld

Dusking

Dusking is the Scar the Dusk leaves (see Scars). It's written on the sheet with its stage, such as Dusking 2, and never heals with rest or downtime. The Dusk takes, but it also gives: each stage brings a Dusk trait.

  • Gaining Dusking: each point of Tenacity damage from the Dusk raises Dusking by 1. Mark the new stage, gain what it brings (below), then make the madness check.
  • Penalty to Bouts: Dusking is subtracted from every Bout of Madness roll, whatever caused it: 3d6 − missing Tenacity − Dusking.
  • The curse of the Dusk: having Dusking 1 or more is what Mendris calls the curse. The formal test detects it from stage 1.
StageEffect
1Nothing visible, but the curse test detects it. Gain the Grey-Weaned Dusk trait.
2Visible. Choose one Dusk quirk and one Dusk trait (below).

Dusk traits

Dusk traits are gifts of the curse. Once you have every trait below, the GM and player invent new ones that fit.

  • Grey-Weaned stage 1 The Dusk has less hold on you. Stress you take from Dusk exposure is reduced by 1.
  • Grey Sight: Your eyes have learned the grey. In the deep Dusklands, you see as if in darkness rather than total darkness (−3d to sight rolls instead of a chance die). Night Sight reduces it to −1d.
  • Hollow Calm: Something in you has gone quiet. Once per day, when you fail to Endure against the Dusk, you take no Stress from it. A Complication or a failure at Deep still deals Tenacity damage.
  • Kindred Dread: Dusktouched and duskborn creatures sense something of their own in you. You get +1d to Stealth to avoid their notice, and the first attack each of them makes against you in a fight takes −1d.
  • Unsettling: People feel the grey on you before they see it. +1d to Intimidation, but anyone who knows the signs recognizes the curse.

Dusk quirks. The GM and player choose or invent one that fits. Each is a sign of the curse and comes with a drawback:

  • Drained colour: your eyes, hair or skin turn grey. Anyone who knows the signs recognizes the curse.
  • Wrong shadow: your shadow lags or moves on its own. Animals shy away from you (−1d to Animal Ken).
  • Cold blood: you're cold to the touch and feel little warmth. −1d to Empathy rolls to comfort or connect.
  • Beacon-shy: a Beacon's light makes your skin crawl. −1d to all rolls while in a Beacon's direct light.
  • Grey dreams: you dream of the Dusk. The first time each day you clear Stress at a rest, clear 1 less.

Death's Marks

Death's Marks are the Scars you take when you Avoid Death. Choose one of these penalties. Each Death's Mark must be a different one, so a character can avoid death at most three times; the fourth is always the last.

  • Permanently reduce your Health by 1.
  • Permanently reduce your Tenacity by 1. Your Stress track shrinks with it.
  • Permanently reduce one attribute by 1. Derived values that use it change too.
  • Cross through the veil of death. You die.

Persistent damage

  • Persistent damage (such as Bleeding) is applied at the end of the affected character's turn, so they always get to act first.
  • Enduring it: before taking the damage, you can Endure. If your successes equal or exceed the number of rounds the effect has left, you take no damage that round. The effect still continues.

Hazards

Common dangers of the world. Settings add their own. Armor doesn't reduce hazard damage.

HazardEffect
Falling1 bashing damage per 3 meters fallen, or lethal if you land on something sharp. Roll Dexterity + Athletics as you fall (no action): each success reduces the damage by 1. A fall of 30 meters or more is fatal unless something breaks it, such as deep water or a canopy.
FireLethal damage at the end of each of your turns in it: 1 for a torch or small fire, 2 for a bonfire, 3 for a burning building or worse.
Drowning and suffocationYou can hold your breath for rounds equal to 5 × your Stamina. After that, take 1 lethal damage at the end of each of your turns until you can breathe.
Extreme cold or heatWithout protection, after hours equal to your Stamina, take 1 bashing damage each hour, or 1 lethal in severe conditions (a blizzard, a desert at noon).
PoisonA poison has a rating from 1 to 5. It deals 1 lethal damage each hour (each round for a fast poison) for intervals equal to its rating. Before each, you can Endure: with successes equal to or above its rating, you take no damage that interval.
Hunger, thirst and sleeplessnessA day without food or water, a night without rest, or a forced march beyond a day's travel each gives 1 stack of Exhausted.
In Duskworld

Exposure

Every place has an exposure level. While characters are in it, they Endure against the Dusk at the interval shown.

LevelWhereEndure everySuccesses needed
NoneBehind the Beacons, in daylight——
FaintOutside after curfew, the edges of the Beacons' reachDay1
HeavyThe Dusklands4 hours2
DeepThe deep Dusklands, duskborn lairsHour3

Enduring against the Dusk rolls Resolve + Composure, not Stamina + Resolve, needing the level's successes. Like any Endure, it costs your reaction in a fight; outside one, it costs nothing.

  • Success: nothing happens.
  • Failure: take Stress equal to the shortfall (the successes you were missing). It's the grey pressing on your mind. Like any Stress, it can overflow into Tenacity damage, but overflow never counts as damage from the Dusk.
  • Complication, or any failure at Deep: also take 1 Tenacity damage from the Dusk (see Dusking).

Duskborn and some kren creations can also deal Tenacity damage from the Dusk with their abilities.

Protection:

  • Grey-Weaned Dusk trait, Dusking 1 Stress from Dusk exposure is reduced by 1.
  • Hollow Calm Dusk trait once per day, a failed check costs no Stress.
  • Wards and the Allmother's light: a wardwright's charm, or light from the Allmother, gives +1d to Endure against the Dusk. Ordinary light (a lamp, a torch, a fire) helps you see, but doesn't hold back the Dusk. A dawnlamp burning Dawnlight keeps the Dusk out entirely, and a dimlamp lowers the exposure level around it.

Dusklands food: each day you eat or drink anything from the Dusklands that hasn't been purified (see the purifier), Endure against the Dusk, needing 1 success. A failure costs exactly 1 Stress, however far short you fall, and a Complication doesn't deal Tenacity damage. It counts as Stress from Dusk exposure, so Grey-Weaned cancels it: anyone with Dusking 1 or more eats freely.

Natural healing

Wounds heal one at a time, least severe first.

DamageHeals
Bashing1 per hour
Lethal1 per rest
Aggravated1 per week of downtime

Longer stretches of free time use the Downtime rules: a week of downtime heals all bashing and lethal damage, and 1 aggravated wound.
