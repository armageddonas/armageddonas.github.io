Defense and reactions

Every blow is contested. You choose how to meet it, and what it costs you.

Defenselower ofDexterityorWits
  • Defending: You contest an attack with a reaction (Dodge or Parry). If you can't or don't react, you still contest it by rolling your Defense. Dodge, Parry and rolled Defense are all defense rolls.
  • Bonuses and penalties: an effect that changes your Defense itself (such as heavy armor's −1) changes every defense roll, twice over on a Dodge. Most bonuses instead add dice to a specific roll and are written that way: "+1d to Parry", "+1d to Dodge", or "+1d to defense rolls" (any of the three, added once).
  • Dodge reaction Roll twice your Defense with the stakes die. A Complication leaves you Prone. An Opportunity lets you Disengage as a free action. Dodging spends your Defense: until the start of your next turn, you can't Parry or roll Defense against attacks, and attacks against you are uncontested.
  • Parry reaction, melee only Roll Defense + Weaponry if you're holding a weapon, or Defense + Brawl if you're unarmed. Parrying an armed attack unarmed takes −2d.
  • Graze: When an attack misses, take 1 Stress to deal 1 damage of one severity lower than the attack's (lethal becomes bashing, aggravated becomes lethal). Bashing attacks can't graze.
  • Reactive Strike reaction When an enemy leaves your melee reach, make a melee attack against them.
  • Feints and mental attacks: Attacks contested by Intelligence + Resolve can't be Dodged or Parried, and the defender doesn't spend a reaction.
