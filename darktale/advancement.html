How characters grow after character creation.

Milestones

The GM calls a milestone when a story arc closes or at a major turning point, usually once per session in a short story and every one or two sessions in a longer one. Every character advances at the same time.

At each milestone, every character:

  1. Gains 1 talent.
  2. Makes 2 choices from the advancement pool.

The talent

Each milestone gives one of:

  • a talent from any vocation you have (its after tag and other prerequisites must be met),
  • a new vocation's key talent, after which its trees are open to you,
  • a further community trait from any community you belong to (its prerequisites must be met), or
  • a new community's key trait (see Joining another community). The GM and player should find an in-world reason for it.

The advancement pool

Each choice marks one box on an option.

OptionBoxesYou gain
Skills☐ ☐ ☐ ☐2 skill dots in different skills rated below 3, or 1 dot in a skill rated 3 or higher
Specialties☐ ☐1 specialty, or 1 improvement to a specialty you have
Training☐ ☐Another talent (as above)
Toughness☐+1 Health
Nerve☐+1 Stress box
Attribute☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐ ☐+1 attribute dot, for boxes equal to its new rating
  • Boxes give their benefit as soon as you mark them, except Attribute.
  • Attribute: mark these boxes with a slash (⧄, like bashing damage). Raising an attribute costs slashed boxes equal to its new rating (2 to raise it to 2, up to 5 to raise it to 5). Once you have enough, cross that many out with an X (☒, like lethal damage) and gain the dot. Slashed boxes you haven't crossed out wait for later, so you can save up across milestones.
  • Full options: once every box of an option is marked (Attribute: slashed or crossed out), you can't choose it again this cycle.
  • New cycle: the pool has 16 boxes, but a cycle is only 10 choices (5 milestones). After your 10th choice, clear the pool and start again. You'll never fill everything in one cycle, so each cycle you decide what to focus on. Slashed Attribute boxes that weren't crossed out stay slashed into the next cycle.

Limits

  • Skills: the creation cap of 3 no longer applies; skills can reach 5.
  • Specialties: each improvement adds +1d, up to +3d and no higher than the skill's permanent dots (see Specialties).
  • Attributes: no attribute can go above 5.

Tiers stay informative only. For example, each 5-milestone cycle could mark a new tier.
