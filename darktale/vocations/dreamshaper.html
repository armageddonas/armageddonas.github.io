Vocation · Duskworld

Dreamshaper

Some kehars carry the blood of the First Dreamers and can reach into the Everdream, the dream that dreamed the world, to make their own dreams real. Their gift is Dreamshaping, and the effects they create are called dreamcrafts.

Playing a dreamshaper

In combat

You confuse and frighten your enemies with figments and nightmares, or shape the ground, a weapon or a dream creature to fight beside you.

In social encounters

You wear another face, push dread or longing into a mind, and at your strongest you can make someone obey.

While exploring

You hide, disguise and deceive, walk in the dreams of people far away, and shape whatever tool the moment needs.

Key talent1 action

Dreamer's Blood

Gain the Dreamshaping power skill and a Reverie pool. Waking Dream: spend 1 Reverie to create a brief image or sound no bigger than you, or a small object with no bulk and no moving parts that's plainly unreal to the touch. It lasts until the end of your next turn. Waking Dream is a nudge, not a full dreamcraft: you don't roll.

Kehars only. Dreamshaper can't be your first vocation: take its key talent with the free pick at character creation, or through advancement. The key talent gives the Dreamshaping power skill and a Reverie pool.

Dreamcrafts

  • Power skill: Dreamshaping. Each dreamcraft names the attribute it's rolled with.
  • Reverie: the pool is 2 + the higher of Wits or Presence. It refills at the end of a rest only if you slept, and at the end of a week of downtime. When it's empty, you can pay with 2 Stress per point, like any power pool. Reverie paid for a dreamcraft that's still active stays reserved until it ends.
  • Always a gamble: every dreamcraft roll raises the stakes.

Control

The roll decides what the dreamcraft costs, not just whether it works.

ResultEffectReverie cost
Success with OpportunityWorksHalf, rounded up
SuccessWorksFull
Failure with OpportunityDoesn't workNone: the Everdream lets you go cleanly
FailureDoesn't work1
Failure with ComplicationDoesn't workHalf, rounded up, and a dream phenomenon breaks loose

A Complication on a successful dreamcraft is an ordinary setback, chosen by the GM.

Materializing

A dreamcraft can be cast in the moment or materialized slowly. The time you take sets the unit it's measured in: it lasts one unit per success.

WayTimeUnitOr instead
In the momentThe actions it listsRounds—
Meditation1 minuteMinutes+1d, lasting rounds
Deep meditation1 hourHours+2d, lasting rounds
DreamedA night's sleepDays+3d, lasting rounds
  • Never quick: casting a dreamcraft in the moment takes at least 2 actions. Shaping a dream is slower than swinging a sword.
  • Longer or stronger: when you materialize slowly, choose either the longer unit or the bonus dice. A dreamcraft with an instant effect always takes the bonus.
  • Sustaining: when a dreamcraft would end, you can pay 1 Reverie to keep it for one more unit, as often as you like. That Reverie is spent as usual; only the dreamcraft's initial cost stays reserved.
  • Dreamed dreamcrafts are paid when you wake, from the Reverie the rest just refilled, and stay reserved for as long as they last: a Dreamveil kept over a hideout for days costs its dreamshaper that much Reverie every day. Some dreamcrafts can only be dreamed, or only be dreamed or deep-meditated.

Phenomena, nightmares and nexuses

  • Dream phenomena: odd breezes, strange noises, objects moving on their own, a sudden chill, runetech flickering out for a few minutes, small things from the Everdream appearing or vanishing. The GM chooses, scaled to the dreamcraft.
  • Nightmares: in Heavy or Deep Dusk exposure, any Complication on a dreamcraft roll turns it into a nightmare, even on a success. The GM twists the effect against you or your allies.
  • Dream Nexuses: some places touch the Everdream. A minor nexus gives +1d to dreamcraft rolls, a major one +2d. At a nexus, any Complication also releases a phenomenon.

Mesmer

Figments, disguises, fear, and the dreams and minds of others.

Mesmerafter Dreamer's Blood

Figments

2 actions
1 Reverie. Roll Wits + Dreamshaping to create an illusion of sight and sound within 10 meters, filling up to one small area per Dreamshaping dot. Moving it takes 1 action. Anyone who touches it, or has a reason to doubt it, rolls Wits + Composure contested by your roll to see through it. A figment can hide you as if you were Obscured, and you can use it to set up anyone it fools.

Mental Awakening

2 actions
1 Reverie. Roll Intelligence + Dreamshaping. Your mind opens to the Everdream: +2d to Mental skill rolls while it lasts.

Dreamguise

2 actions · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Roll Intelligence + Dreamshaping to disguise yourself, or a willing creature you touch, as anyone of about the same size. Anyone who doubts the disguise contests it with Wits + Composure. The disguised character gets +1d to Subterfuge to pass as that person.

Dread

2 actions · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Push a nightmare into the mind of someone within 10 meters: roll Manipulation + Dreamshaping, contested by Wits + Composure. On a success, they're Frightened of you while the dreamcraft lasts.

Dreamwalk

Dreamshaping 3+
Dreamed only. 2 Reverie. Roll Resolve + Dreamshaping and choose up to one sleeper per Dreamshaping dot, each someone whose face and name you know, within 10 kilometers per dot. While it lasts, whenever you sleep you can enter one of their dreams and speak with them freely; joining another means leaving the first. An unwilling dreamer contests with Intelligence + Resolve each time you enter. Your presence lingers: +1d to your next Social roll against them.

Dreamsteal

Dreamshaping 3+
Dreamed or deep meditation only. 3 Reverie, instant. Choose someone you know within 10 kilometers per Dreamshaping dot: roll Manipulation + Dreamshaping, contested by Intelligence + Resolve. On a success, their next rest gives them nothing (no Stress cleared, no healing, nothing refreshed) and they gain 1 stack of Exhausted. Most dreamshapers find it vile.

Dreamveil

Dreamshaping 4+
Dreamed or deep meditation only. 4 Reverie. Roll Intelligence + Dreamshaping. A building, or a camp up to 20 meters across, looks and sounds empty and ordinary from outside. Finding it means beating your roll with Wits + Investigation. Hidden kehar settlements are kept this way.

Enthrall

2 actions · Dreamshaping 4+
3 Reverie. Roll Manipulation + Dreamshaping, contested by Intelligence + Resolve. On a success, the target obeys one short command while the dreamcraft lasts, following the compulsion rules.

Figments, disguises, fear, and the dreams of others. Mesmer dreamcrafts mostly roll Wits, Intelligence or Manipulation.

Maker

Making dreams real, from dreamstuff objects to dream creatures and, at the last, reality itself.

Makerafter Dreamer's Blood

Dreamstuff

2 actions
1 Reverie. Roll Intelligence + Dreamshaping to shape a simple object of up to bulk 1: a rope, a tool, a key blank, or a weapon that counts as Light. Nothing with intricate moving parts. A tool works as a kit with charges equal to your successes, up to 3.

Reshape

2 actions · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Roll Intelligence + Dreamshaping to change one property of a non-living object you touch, up to bulk 1 per Dreamshaping dot: its shape, hardness or texture. Make a broken item work, soften a lock until it opens (your roll against the lock), or raise handholds in a wall.

Dream Companion

2 actions · Dreamshaping 3+
3 Reverie. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping to call a creature from the Everdream. It's a companion with a rating of your Dreamshaping − 1, proficient in what you choose. It dissolves when the dreamcraft ends or its Health fills. You can have only one at a time.

Shape Ground

2 actions · Dreamshaping 3+
2 Reverie. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping. Within 10 meters, turn a small area into difficult terrain, smooth difficult terrain away, or raise a wall that gives full cover.

Unmake

2 actions · Dreamshaping 4+
3 Reverie, instant. Roll Intelligence + Dreamshaping to return a non-living object of up to bulk 1 per Dreamshaping dot to the Everdream. A Device contests with its own roll. Against another dreamshaper's dreamcraft or a creature of the Everdream, it's contested by their dreamcraft roll or rating; if you win, it ends.

Rewrite

3 actions · Dreamshaping 4+
5 Reverie, once per day. The world is only another dream. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping to change one thing about your surroundings within 10 meters, with the GM's approval: a fall becomes a slow drift, a door becomes a wall, a fire becomes cold smoke. Creatures it affects contest with Dexterity + Stamina. When the dreamcraft ends, so does the change.

After Dream Companion or Unmake

Also available after Unmake

Rewrite listed above ↑

Making dreams real. Maker dreamcrafts mostly roll Intelligence or Presence.
