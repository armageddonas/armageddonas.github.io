Vocation · Duskworld

Dreamshaper

Some kehars carry the blood of the First Dreamers and can reach into the Everdream, the dream that dreamed the world, to make their own dreams real. Their gift is Dreamshaping, and the effects they create are called dreamcrafts.

Playing a dreamshaper

In combat

You confuse and frighten your enemies with figments and nightmares, or shape the ground, a weapon or a dream creature to fight beside you.

In social encounters

You wear another face, push dread or longing into a mind, and at your strongest you can make someone obey.

While exploring

You hide, disguise and deceive, walk in the dreams of people far away, and shape whatever tool the moment needs.

Key talent

Dreamer's Blood

Gain the Dreamshaping power skill and a Reverie pool. Once per day, you can nap for an hour and regain Reverie equal to your Dreamshaping dots. You also gain one dreamcraft that comes after the key talent, from either tree.

Kehars only. Dreamshaper can't be your first vocation: take its key talent with the free pick at character creation, or through advancement. The key talent gives the Dreamshaping power skill and a Reverie pool.

In Mendris

What a dreamshaper is likely to be in Mendris.

  • A kehar dream-reader telling fortunes in the markets. Dreamshaper (Mesmer)
  • A healer of nightmares on the Frontiers. Dreamshaper (Mesmer), often with Counselor (Faithful)
  • A Syndicate illusionist. Dreamshaper (Mesmer or Maker)

Two vocations together:

  • A dream-confessor who eases what people bring back from the Dusklands. Dreamshaper (Mesmer) + Counselor (Faithful)

Dreamcrafts

  • Power skill: Dreamshaping. Each dreamcraft names the attribute it's rolled with.
  • Reverie: the pool is 2 + the higher of Wits or Presence + your Dreamshaping dots, so it grows with your power. It refills at the end of a rest only if you slept, and at the end of a week of downtime. Between rests, a nap (from Dreamer's Blood) restores some. When it's empty, you can pay with 2 Stress per point, like any power pool. Reverie paid for a dreamcraft that's still active stays reserved until it ends.
  • Always a gamble: every dreamcraft roll raises the stakes, and the stakes die replaces one die of your pool: the dream is never fully yours to control. If your pool is down to a chance die, roll the chance die alone.

Control

The roll decides what the dreamcraft costs, not just whether it works.

ResultEffectReverie cost
Success with OpportunityWorksHalf, rounded up
SuccessWorksFull
FailureDoesn't workNone: the Everdream lets you go cleanly
Failure with ComplicationDoesn't work1, and a dream phenomenon breaks loose

A Complication on a successful dreamcraft is an ordinary setback, chosen by the GM.

Materializing

Each dreamcraft lists how long it takes to materialize, beside its other tags: its actions, 1 minute, 1 hour or a rest. That's the fastest it can be materialized. Materialized in that time, it lasts one round. Each step slower than its listed time lengthens the unit by one step: minutes, then hours, then days. Extra successes and Empowered effects work as for any power (see Power pools): each success after the first buys an option from the dreamcraft's list or one more unit.

The steps, from fastest to slowest: the actions the dreamcraft lists, 1 minute, 1 hour and a rest, which you spend dreaming it.

Steps slower than listedUnitOr instead
0Rounds—
1Minutes+1d, lasting rounds
2Hours+2d, lasting rounds
3Days+3d, lasting rounds
  • The slowest step: a rest is as slow as a dreamcraft can be materialized, so a dreamcraft that takes a rest lasts rounds, unless it says otherwise. One that takes 1 minute can reach hours at most, one that takes 1 hour minutes at most. Powerful dreamcrafts take longer to materialize, so they can't be stretched as far.
  • Never quick: a dreamcraft materialized in actions takes at least 2. Shaping a dream is slower than swinging a sword.
  • Longer or stronger: when you take longer than listed, choose either the longer unit or the bonus dice. A dreamcraft with an instant effect always takes the bonus.
  • Sustaining: when a dreamcraft would end, you can pay 1 Reverie to keep it for one more unit, as often as you like. That Reverie is spent as usual; only the dreamcraft's initial cost stays reserved.
  • Dreamcrafts materialized over a rest are paid when you wake, from the Reverie the rest just refilled, and stay reserved for as long as they last: a Dreamveil kept over a hideout for days costs its dreamshaper that much Reverie every day.

Phenomena, nightmares and nexuses

  • Dream phenomena: odd breezes, strange noises, objects moving on their own, a sudden chill, runetech flickering out for a few minutes, small things from the Everdream appearing or vanishing. The GM chooses, scaled to the dreamcraft.
  • Nightmares: in Heavy or Deep Dusk exposure, any Complication on a dreamcraft roll turns it into a nightmare, even on a success. The GM twists the effect against you or your allies.
  • Dream Nexuses: some places touch the Everdream. A minor nexus gives +1d to dreamcraft rolls, a major one +2d. At a nexus, any Complication also releases a phenomenon.

Mesmer

Figments, disguises, fear, and the dreams and minds of others.

Mesmerafter Dreamer's Blood

Figments

2 actions
1 Reverie. Roll Wits + Dreamshaping to create an illusion of sight and sound within 10 meters, filling up to one small area per Dreamshaping dot. Moving it takes 1 action. Anyone who touches it, or has a reason to doubt it, rolls Wits + Resolve contested by your roll to see through it. A figment can hide you as if you were Obscured, and you can use it to set up anyone it fools.
  • Extra successes: add a sense (smell, warmth, a breeze); rolls to see through it take −1d (once); it follows a simple script without your actions (a guard pacing, a crowd murmuring); or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+1 Reverie): it survives a touch: the first touch doesn't give anyone a reason to doubt it. It still can't harm anyone.

Mental Awakening

1 minute
1 Reverie. Roll Intellect + Dreamshaping. Your mind opens to the Everdream: +2d to Mental skill rolls while it lasts.
  • Extra successes: ignore the untrained penalty on one Mental skill; the bonus becomes +3d; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+1 Reverie): perfect recall. While it lasts, you remember anything you've once seen or read, exactly as it was.

Dreamguise

1 minute · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Roll Intellect + Dreamshaping to disguise yourself, or a willing creature you touch, as anyone of about the same size. Anyone who doubts the disguise contests it with Wits + Resolve. The disguised character gets +1d to Manipulation to pass as that person.
  • Extra successes: the voice matches too; the apparent size changes by a step (a child, a tall adult); rolls to see through it take −1d (once); or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): it holds against touch and close inspection: only magic that reveals hidden effects shows it.

Dread

2 actions · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Push a nightmare into the mind of someone within 10 meters: roll Presence + Dreamshaping, contested by Wits + Resolve. On a success, they're Frightened of you while the dreamcraft lasts.
  • Extra successes: they're also Disoriented until the end of their next turn; they take 1 Stress at the start of each of their turns; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): one more target within 10 meters per two Dreamshaping dots, rounded up. Each contests separately.

Dreamwalk

rest · Dreamshaping 3+
2 Reverie. It lasts minutes, not rounds. Roll Resolve + Dreamshaping and choose up to one sleeper per Dreamshaping dot, each someone whose face and name you know, within 10 kilometers per Dreamshaping dot. While it lasts, you can enter one of their dreams and speak with them freely; joining another means leaving the first. An unwilling dreamer contests with Intellect + Resolve each time you enter.
  • Extra successes: one more sleeper; you shape the dream's setting; you learn one thing they dream of, a fear or a desire; or +1 minute.
  • Empowered (+1 Reverie): you can reach someone you know only by face, or only by name.

Dreamsteal

1 hour · Dreamshaping 3+
3 Reverie, instant. Choose someone you know within 10 kilometers per Dreamshaping dot: roll Presence + Dreamshaping, contested by Intellect + Resolve. On a success, their next rest gives them nothing (no Stress cleared, no healing, nothing refreshed) and they gain 1 stack of Exhausted. Most dreamshapers find it vile.
  • Extra successes: they gain 1 more stack of Exhausted (once); they don't know it was done to them.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): you take what they lose: at your next rest, you clear 1 extra Stress and regain 1 Reverie.

After Dreamwalk or Dread

Also available after Dread

Dreamveil

1 hour · Dreamshaping 4+
4 Reverie. It lasts hours, not rounds, or days if materialized over a rest. Roll Intellect + Dreamshaping. An area up to 10 meters across per Dreamshaping dot, centered on you, looks and sounds empty and ordinary from outside: a house, a yard, a camp. Finding it means beating your roll with Wits + Investigation. Hidden kehar settlements are kept this way.
  • Extra successes: +10 meters across (repeatable); rolls to find it take −1d (once); or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): it turns people away. Anyone who comes near without knowing it's there takes −2d to search near it and feels drawn elsewhere. It's a nudge, not a compulsion.

Enthrall

2 actions · Dreamshaping 4+
3 Reverie. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping, contested by Intellect + Resolve, against someone within 10 meters who can hear you. On a success, they obey one short command while the dreamcraft lasts, following the compulsion rules. Materialized more slowly, it lasts longer: they have more time to carry out the command, but it takes you longer to give it.
  • Extra successes: a second command once the first is done (once); when it ends, they don't realize they were compelled, an exception to the compulsion rules; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): a fuller command: a few sentences of instruction instead of one.

Maker

Making dreams real, from dreamstuff objects to dream creatures and, at the last, reality itself.

Makerafter Dreamer's Blood

Dreamstuff

2 actions
1 Reverie. Roll Intellect + Dreamshaping to shape a simple object of up to bulk 1 in your hand: a rope, a tool, a key blank, or a weapon that counts as Light. Nothing with intricate moving parts. A tool works as a kit with 1 charge.
  • Extra successes: up to bulk 2 (once); one more charge as a kit (up to 3); or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+1 Reverie): any one-handed weapon, not just a Light one, or a tool with moving parts.

Mend

2 actions
1 Reverie. Touch a broken, worn or damaged object and roll Intellect + Dreamshaping. While it lasts, it works as if whole: a snapped blade cuts, a torn coat keeps out the rain, and armor ignores its Wear. When it ends, the damage is back.
  • Extra successes: clear 1 Wear for real (once); a broken Device works; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+1 Reverie): the repair is real, and stays when the dreamcraft ends, for an object of bulk 1 or less.

Reshape

2 actions · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Roll Intellect + Dreamshaping to change one property of a non-living object you touch, up to bulk 1 per Dreamshaping dot: its shape, hardness or texture. Make a broken item work, soften a lock until it opens (your roll against the lock), or raise handholds in a wall.
  • Extra successes: change one more property (repeatable); +1 bulk (repeatable); or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): you can reshape an object you can see within 10 meters, instead of touching it.

After Dreamstuff or Mend

Also available after Mend

Dreamlight

2 actions · Dreamshaping 2+
2 Reverie. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping. A light of dreamstuff fills a small area within 10 meters with ordinary light. It hangs in place, or follows you.
  • Extra successes: +10 meters of reach; one more small area (repeatable); its light is bright, as daylight; it moves as you direct, without your actions; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+1 Reverie): it dazzles. One creature within 10 meters that looks at it contests your roll with Wits + Resolve, or is Blinded until the end of its next turn.

Dream Companion

1 minute · Dreamshaping 3+
3 Reverie. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping to call a creature from the Everdream, appearing within 10 meters. It's a companion with a rating of your Dreamshaping − 1, proficient in what its kind of creature does (see the companion rules; the GM decides). It dissolves when the dreamcraft ends or its Health fills. You can have only one at a time.
  • Extra successes: +1 rating (repeatable, up to your Dreamshaping); +2 Health; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): it takes one action on each of your turns without you spending yours.

Shape Ground

2 actions · Dreamshaping 3+
2 Reverie. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping. Within 10 meters, turn a small area into difficult terrain, smooth difficult terrain away, or raise a wall 3 meters long and as tall as a person that gives full cover.
  • Extra successes: one more small area, or 3 more meters of wall (repeatable); a gap or a door in the wall; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): the ground grasps: creatures in the area are Immobilized until they break free with Might + Athletics against your roll.

Unmake

2 actions · Dreamshaping 4+
3 Reverie. Roll Intellect + Dreamshaping to return a non-living object you touch, of up to bulk 1 per Dreamshaping dot, to the Everdream. While it lasts, the object is gone from the world; when it ends, it returns where it was, or to the nearest open space. Someone holding or carrying it contests with Intellect + Resolve, holding on to what's real; a Device contests with its own roll instead, if that's higher. Against another dreamshaper's dreamcraft or a creature of the Everdream, it's contested by their dreamcraft roll or rating; if you win, it ends.
  • Extra successes: +1 bulk (repeatable); or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+2 Reverie): you can unmake part of something larger: a hole in a wall, a door from its frame.

Rewrite

3 actions · Dreamshaping 4+
5 Reverie, once per day. The world is only another dream. Roll Presence + Dreamshaping to change one thing about your surroundings within 10 meters, with the GM's approval: a fall becomes a slow drift, a door becomes a wall, a fire becomes cold smoke. Creatures it affects contest with Agility + Endurance. When the dreamcraft ends, so does the change.
  • Extra successes: one more change (once); rolls to contest it take −1d; or +1 unit.
  • Empowered (+3 Reverie): it reaches 20 meters.

After Dream Companion or Unmake

Also available after Unmake
