Vocation

Scholar

Scholars treat knowledge as a tool. Their key talent, Erudition, gives them expertise they can reassign after a rest with access to research, so they can prepare for whatever comes next.

Vocation skills
Academics, Crafts or MedicineAt character creation, put 1 skill dot in one of them.
Starting gear
A knife or club, no armor, and a medical kit or tool kit.

Playing a scholar

In combat

You plan and support. You study the enemy, set up your allies, heal the wounded or bring a crafted Device to bear.

In social encounters

You know the facts others don't, and some scholars learn to read people as well as books.

While exploring

You identify, research, repair and treat. You're the one who knows what that machine does and how to patch up the party at a rest.

Key talent

Erudition

Gain a temporary specialty and +1 dot in two Mental skills. Reassign them at the end of a rest with access to research (a library, archives, the internet).

Engineer

Crafting, field repairs and Devices.

Engineerafter Erudition

Efficient Engineer

Crafts 2+
Gain a Crafts (Weapon Crafting, Armor Crafting or Equipment Crafting) specialty. When crafting, your Opportunity range expands by 1 and material costs are halved.

Field Repair

2 actions · Crafts 2+
Once per minute, roll Intelligence + Crafts (or Technology, for a Device), needing 2 successes, to clear Wear from an ally's armor up to your Crafts dots, make a broken item work until the end of the fight, or restore 1 charge to a Device.

Deep Study

passive
Erudition gives you one more specialty and two more skill dots.

Inventive Design

Technology 3+
Gain a Crafts (Device Crafting) specialty. You can craft Devices rated 1 higher than usual.

Fine Handiwork

No prerequisite
When crafting, advanced upgrades cost the same as ordinary upgrades.

Overcharge

Technology 4+
Once per turn, as a free action, push a Device close to you: raise the stakes on its next roll. On an Opportunity, it acts again right away at −2d; that roll can't trigger Overcharge.

Master Artisan

No prerequisite
When crafting, you get 2 extra attempts and the interval is halved.

Overwhelm with Details

Academics 4+
Take 2 Stress to use Academics in place of another skill on a Mental or Social roll.

Strategist

Planning and set-ups that turn a fight, including extra actions for the whole party.

Strategistafter Erudition

Strategize

Investigation 2+
After a set-up action using an Erudition skill, you can give the +1d to an ally instead. Take 2 Stress to also stop the target from reacting against that ally.

Mind and Body

passive
Gain a weapon specialty and one more Erudition skill. Erudition can now pick Physical skills.

Composed

passive
+2 maximum Stress.

Know Your Moment

passive · Investigation 3+
+1d to defense rolls from the start of each round until you take your turn.

Deep Contemplation

2 actions · Academics 3+
Reassign up to 2 of your Erudition skills or specialties.

Keen Insight

passive
When your set-up action against a target succeeds, they also get −1d on their next roll.

Contingency

reaction · Academics 4+
Take 2 Stress to remove a Complication from the roll of an ally close to you.

Turning Point

2 actions · Investigation 4+
Once per minute, take 2 Stress and roll Wits + Investigation, contested by the enemy leader's Intelligence + Resolve (or the strongest enemy's, if there's no leader), with +1d if you took a slow turn. On a success, you and your allies gain 1 extra action on your next turns.

Surgeon

Healing in the fight and at rest, and reviving the fallen.

Surgeonafter Erudition

Field Medicine

1 action · Medicine 2+
Take 2 Stress and roll Intelligence + Medicine, needing 2 successes. On a success, a conscious character close to you heals 2 damage, least severe first, and stops Bleeding.

Emotional Intelligence

passive
Gain a Medicine (Diagnosis) specialty and one more Erudition skill. Erudition can now pick Social skills.

Anatomical Insight

No prerequisite
Once per round, when you hit with an unarmed attack, take 1 Stress or spend an Opportunity to give the target 1 stack of Exhausted. Stacks from this talent wear off after one minute.

Collected

passive
+1d to resist Mental and Social rolls.

Swift Healer

Medicine 3+
Field Medicine becomes a free action and heals 1 extra damage.

Applied Medicine

passive · Academics 3+
Your Field Medicine heals 1 extra damage.

Ongoing Care

Academics 4+
Gain a Medicine (Mental Health Care) specialty. When you Recover a patient, they also lose one condition caused by injury, such as Bleeding, Slowed or 1 stack of Exhausted.

Resuscitation

Medicine 4+
Field Medicine can revive a character who is Unconscious, or who died from damage within the last round, for 3 Stress instead of 2. They heal the usual amount, aggravated damage first if their track is full of it, and wake up. It can't undo death from a Bout of Madness or a Death's Mark.
