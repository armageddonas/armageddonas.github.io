Vocation

Agent

Agents live by their wits and their luck: investigators, spies and thieves who turn risk into opportunity. Their key talent lets them reroll the stakes die, so they thrive when the stakes are high.

Vocation skills
Investigation, Subterfuge or ThieveryAt character creation, put 1 skill dot in one of them.
Starting gear
One Light or Discreet weapon (knife, sap or rapier), light armor, and thieves' tools or a disguise kit.

Playing an agent

In combat

You avoid a fair fight. You land cheap shots, set up your allies, stun or silence a target, and slip away when it turns against you.

In social encounters

You lie, disguise yourself, read motives and work your contacts. Getting the truth out of someone, or hiding it, is your job.

While exploring

You open locks, sneak past guards, find clues and spot what others miss.

Key talent

Opportunist

Once per round, reroll your stakes die.

Investigator

Uncovering motives and truths, and wearing targets down with mental pressure.

Investigatorafter Opportunist

Watchful Eye

reaction · Investigation 2+
Use Opportunist on the stakes die of a willing ally close to you. This counts as your use of Opportunist this round.

Get 'Em Talking

2 actions · Empathy 3+
Take 1 Stress and roll Wits + Investigation, contested by Wits + Composure, to learn the target's motivation. For the next hour, you can raise the stakes on rolls that leverage it.

Quick Analysis

free action · Investigation 3+
Once per round, take 2 Stress to gain 2 extra actions this turn, usable only for Mental rolls to use a skill, take a set-up action or use an Agent talent that isn't an attack. These can repeat actions you've already taken, but set-ups against the same target don't stack.

Baleful

passive
When you win a contested Mental or Social roll against a target, they also take 1 Stress.

Gather Evidence

Empathy 3+
Gain an Academics (Law) specialty. Once per round, when you succeed on a Mental roll against a target, you become Determined.

Hardy

passive
+2 Health.

Sleuth's Instincts

Investigation 4+
+1d on Mental rolls against characters whose motivation you know. You know when those characters lie to you.

Close the Case

3 actions · Investigation 4+
Take 3 Stress and roll Intelligence + Investigation, contested by Intelligence + Resolve, with +1d if you know the target's motivation. On a success, they must answer one question truthfully or concede the point publicly, their choice, and they take −2d on rolls against you for one hour. On a failure, they get +1d on their next roll against you.

Spy

False identities, disguises and silent takedowns.

Spyafter Opportunist

Sure Outcome

Empathy 3+
When you use Opportunist, take 2 Stress to turn an Opportunity into a Complication that counts as 2 successes instead of 1, or turn any Complication into an Opportunity.

Plausible Excuse

reaction · Subterfuge 2+
Gain a Thievery (Sleight of Hand) specialty. When you're caught skulking, take 2 Stress to feign innocence.

Collected

passive
+1d to resist Mental and Social rolls.

Cover Story

passive
Gain a false identity and a Socialize specialty in its culture or group.

Subtle Takedown

2 actions · Empathy 4+
Make an unarmed attack with Dexterity + Empathy against an unsuspecting target, raising the stakes. On a hit, you and the target are Grappled (see Grappling), and while you hold them, they can't speak or call out.

Mighty

passive
When you hit with a weapon or unarmed attack, deal +1 damage per action spent on the attack.

Mercurial Facade

Subterfuge 4+
Disguise yourself with Subterfuge, without physical supplies. The first character to see through the disguise is Surprised.

High Society Contacts

patron in high society
Gain a Socialize (High Society) specialty. Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a roll to interact in high society.

Thief

Risk-taking, quick hands, extra actions and the criminal underworld.

Thiefafter Opportunist

Risky Behavior

Empathy 3+
Take 1 Stress to raise the stakes on your roll.

Cheap Shot

1 action
Take 1 Stress and make an unarmed attack with Dexterity + Thievery, raising the stakes. On a hit, the target is Disoriented until the end of their next turn; on an Opportunity, they're Stunned instead.

Double Down

No prerequisite
You can use Opportunist a second time on the same roll. This is an exception to the one-reroll rule. If that reroll shows a Complication, take 2 Stress.

Surefooted

passive
+3 Speed. Reduce damage from falls and dangerous terrain by your Athletics dots.

Underworld Contacts

patron in the criminal underworld
Gain a Streetwise (Criminal Groups) specialty. Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a Social roll against criminals.

Shadow Step

free action · Thievery 4+
After you disengage, take 2 Stress and roll Dexterity + Thievery against each enemy's Wits + Composure to hide from them. +1d if you're in cover or obscured.

Fast Talker

free action · Empathy 4+
Once per round, take 2 Stress to gain 2 extra actions this turn, usable only for Social rolls to use a skill, take a set-up action or use an Agent talent that isn't an attack. These can repeat actions you've already taken, but set-ups against the same target don't stack.

Trickster's Hand

free action · Thievery 4+
Once per round, take 2 Stress to gain 2 extra actions this turn, usable only for Physical rolls to use a skill, take a set-up action or use an Agent talent that isn't an attack. These can repeat actions you've already taken, but set-ups against the same target don't stack.
