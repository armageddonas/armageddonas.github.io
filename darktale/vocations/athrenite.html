Athrenite

Athrenites are humans who carry a power that isn't theirs, and know it by their orange blood. SPEAR infuses its chosen with it in sealed laboratories, so that the power that once served the Allmother's murderers now serves Mendris. Some say it came from the First Followers' blasphemy; some say the Allmother allows it, others that she curses it. No one knows, and few agree. The power is called Athren, and each athrenite belongs to one Form of it. The power is fast and strong, and it's always hungry: use it hard enough and it consumes you, and you become a Transcendent.

Playing an athrenite

In combat

Depending on your Form, you harden into living armor, forge crystal weapons from your will, rot flesh with a touch, twist your body into a monster's, tear at minds, or step through the Veil.

In social encounters

You hear surface thoughts and speak mind to mind, feel the last moments of the dead, see the auras of the gifted, or simply loom.

While exploring

You see far-off places, move things without touching them, hide in chameleon skin, step through the Otherside, and stand where others would break.

Key talent1 action

Orange Blood

Gain the Athren power skill and an Ichor pool, and choose your Form: Guardian, Harbinger, Listener, Mawbound, Shardbound or Veilwalker. Your Form never changes. You can take talents only from your Form's tree and from Vessel. Surge: once per round, as a free action, spend 1 Ichor to add +1d to a Physical roll.

Humans only. Athrenite can't be your first vocation: take its key talent with the free pick at character creation, or through advancement. The key talent gives the Athren power skill and an Ichor pool.

Athren

  • Power skill: Athren. Each power names the attribute it's rolled with.
  • Ichor: the pool is 2 + the higher of Stamina or Resolve + twice your Athren dots, so it grows with your power. Running dry is rarely what stops an athrenite; the Athren Stage is. It refills at the end of a rest and at the end of a week of downtime. When it's empty, you can pay with 2 Stress per point, like any power pool.
  • Fast: evoking any power takes 1 action, whatever it costs, unless it says it's a reaction.
  • Paid in full: you pay a power's cost whether or not the roll succeeds. Athren doesn't give back what you spend.
  • Duration: a power lasts one minute per success unless it says otherwise. You can sustain it for 1 Ichor per extra unit; its initial cost stays reserved while it lasts.
  • Attacks: an attack a power gives you (a bite, claws, a crystal weapon, Deathtouch) works like a weapon: its listed damage is the weapon's damage, added to your net successes as usual (see Attacks).
  • Overcharge: before rolling, you can spend extra Ichor on a power: +1d per extra point.
  • Your threshold is your Athren dots. Spending more Ichor than that on a single power, Overcharge included, risks your Athren Stage.

Born and made

Most athrenites are made: infused in SPEAR's laboratories, registered, and bound to SPEAR's service for as long as they're useful. Some are born, carrying orange blood from birth and awakening to their power later, often violently. SPEAR denies they exist. Officially, an athrenite SPEAR didn't make is unregistered, and so illegal; in practice, SPEAR finds them and either recruits them or makes them disappear.

Both use the same rules. Choose at character creation:

  • Made: you have a SPEAR record, a handler, and a debt the Houses won't let you forget. Your papers say what you are.
  • Born: you have a secret. Orange blood is hard to hide when you bleed, and your power will draw attention sooner or later.

Mawbound are the exception: a made athrenite who turns out Mawbound is a failure, and SPEAR destroys its failures early. Almost every Mawbound is born, or a fugitive who escaped the laboratories (see Mawbound).

Athren Stage

The Athren Stage is a Scar written with its stage, from 0 to 4, such as Athren Stage 2. It goes down only through the ways in Settling the blood, and never from stage 4.

  • Risking the stage: when an Athren roll shows a Complication, or when you spend more Ichor on one power than your threshold, roll Resolve + Composure. You need successes equal to the stage you'd reach: 1 to reach stage 1, up to 4 to reach stage 4. On a failure, your Athren Stage rises by 1.
  • Stories can raise it too: a failed infusion, a SPEAR "treatment", drinking something you shouldn't.
StageEffect
1Surging: once per hour, evoke a power for 1 Ichor less (minimum 1).
2Evolved: every power you evoke uses its Evolved effect, and costs 1 more Ichor.
3Your Form's struggle turns: your Form's struggle gains its stage 3 effect (see each Form).
4Transcendence: you become your Form's Transcendent. The character leaves play and becomes the GM's.

Settling the blood

The power can be pushed back down, but only slowly, and never for free.

  • Only in downtime: your Athren Stage can drop by at most 1 per week of downtime, whatever ways you use. Nothing brings anyone back from stage 4.
  • Stillblood: SPEAR's elixir, brewed in its laboratories. Drink a dose at the start of a week of downtime: at the end of the week, your Athren Stage drops by 1. That week your Ichor doesn't refill and you can't evoke powers. Made athrenites get doses from their handlers, as reward for good service or withheld as leverage. Anyone else needs a favor with SPEAR or House Callarain, or the black market.
  • Black-market stillblood: stolen or diluted doses, 2 slabs each, found with a Manipulation + Streetwise roll. Roll a die when you drink one: on a 1, it was cut with something else and does nothing.
  • Bloodletting: the oldest trick of the born. A helper drains the orange blood from you, as a downtime action: they roll Intelligence + Medicine, needing successes equal to your Athren Stage. You take lethal damage equal to your Athren Stage, which heals normally. On a success, your Athren Stage drops by 1 at the end of the week. On a Complication, someone notices the orange blood, or keeps it.
  • Burning off: go somewhere no one will see and empty the power, again and again, for a week. It takes the whole week, and you can take no other downtime actions. At the end of it, roll Resolve + Composure, needing successes equal to your Athren Stage + 1. On a success, it drops by 1.

Transcendents

What an athrenite becomes when the power finally takes them. Everyone in Mendris has seen at least one: the Vakens, at least, are hired out by the rich.

GuardianVaken: a body near indestructible, like bark, and a mind gone numb. Vakens belong to the Allmother and are lent or rented out as guards, police and beasts of burden. They act only on orders, and only those trained to reach them, the puppeteers, can give them reliably.
HarbingerUnpassed: they reached the threshold of death and couldn't cross. Mindless undead, stuck between life and death.
