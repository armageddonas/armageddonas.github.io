Vocation

Hunter

Hunters track, study and bring down a chosen quarry. Patient and precise, they're as comfortable in the wild as in a crowded street, as long as they know who they're after.

Vocation skills
Survival, Stealth or RangedAt character creation, put 1 skill dot in one of them.
Starting gear
A bow or crossbow and a knife, light armor, and a survival kit or climbing gear.

Playing a hunter

In combat

You pick a quarry and focus on it. You strike from range or from hiding, set traps, and finish what your allies start.

In social encounters

You watch more than you talk. You read a target, follow them unseen and learn their weaknesses before they know you're there.

While exploring

You lead the way. You track, forage, scout and lay traps, often with an animal companion at your side.

In Duskworld

Hunters in Mendris

  • A Duskland hunter bringing back hides and kren salvage. Hunter (Tracker)
  • A Frontier tracker. Hunter (Tracker or Sharpshooter)
  • A bounty hunter working for the Syndicate. Hunter (Assassin or Tracker)
  • A SPEAR heretic-hunter, running down unregistered athrenites and every other abomination that wields power without the Allmother's blessing. Hunter (Magekiller)

Two vocations together:

  • One of SPEAR's leashed heretics, a sanctioned power set loose on the unsanctioned ones. Hunter (Magekiller) + Athrenite (Guardian or Listener)
  • A salvager who maps abandoned kren factories in search of true glints. Hunter (Tracker) + Scholar (Engineer)
Key talent1 action

Seek Quarry

Choose a character you can see, or whose trail or traces you've found, as your quarry. You get +1d on rolls to find, attack or study them. They stay your quarry until you choose another.

Sharpshooter

Aimed ranged attacks, with any ranged weapon, that mark a quarry and keep it under fire.

Sharpshooterafter Seek Quarry

Tagging Shot

2 actions · Wits 3+
Move up to half your Speed, rounded up, and make a ranged attack. On a hit or a graze, the target becomes your quarry.

Combat Training

passive
Gain a weapon specialty and a Socialize (Military Life) specialty, and ignore the penalties of one armor type. Once per round, you can graze without taking Stress.

Sharp Eye

1 action
Observe a target and roll Wits + Investigation, contested by Intelligence + Resolve. On a success, learn their rating, one of their proficiencies, or whether their Health, Stress or power pool is below half.

Steady Aim

1 action · Dexterity 2+
Your next ranged attack this turn has its range increased by half and deals +2 damage.

Exploit Weakness

free action · Wits 4+
Take a set-up action against your quarry without spending an action.

Backstep

free action · Dexterity 3+
After a ranged attack, take 2 Stress to disengage without spending an action. Brace if you reach cover or an obscured area.

Unrelenting Salvo

passive · Dexterity 4+
You can attack your quarry more than once per turn with the same ranged weapon. Each attack costs an action and takes the usual penalty for extra attacks.

Hardy

passive
+2 Health.

Assassin

Stealth and knives. Surprises unsuspecting targets and finishes them quickly.

Assassinafter Seek Quarry

Startling Blow

1 action · Stealth 2+
Take 2 Stress and make an attack. On a hit, the target is Surprised until the end of their next turn, as if ambushed. A character can be startled this way once per minute.

Killing Edge

passive · Wits 3+
Gain Weaponry (Knives) and Ranged (Thrown Weapons) specialties. These weapons gain the Deadly and Quickdraw traits, and you can attack with them using Dexterity instead of Strength.

Fatal Thrust

2 actions · Wits 4+
Attack an unsuspecting target with a Light melee weapon. Deal +2 damage, and gain +2d if the weapon is Discreet. On an Opportunity, choose one: the target is Bleeding for 3 turns, Slowed for one minute, or Stunned until the end of their next turn.

Shadowing

Stealth 3+
Your quarry gets −1d to notice you, and you get +1d to avoid their notice. When your Stealth beats someone's Wits + Composure while you're in cover or obscured, take 3 Stress to make them your quarry.

Mighty

passive
When you hit with a weapon or unarmed attack, deal +1 damage per action spent on the attack.

Cold Eyes

passive
When you defeat your quarry, choose a new quarry without spending an action. Once per day, you also clear 1 Stress.

Swift Strikes

1 action · Weaponry, Brawl or Ranged 3+ (the weapon's skill)
Take 2 Stress to make an extra attack with the same weapon (see Extra attacks). This is an exception to the one-attack rule.

Sidestep

Stealth 4+
Your first Dodge each round doesn't spend your Defense, while you're not wearing armor rated 2 or higher.

Weapon traits (Deadly, Quickdraw, Discreet, Light) are defined in Core Rules.

Magekiller

A mundane hunter of the gifted, who senses magic being used and punishes it.

Magekillerafter Seek Quarry

Seek the Gifted

reaction · Wits 2+
When a character you can see uses a power (spends from a power pool or pays its cost in Stress), take 1 Stress to make them your quarry. +1d to notice your quarry while it's near you.

Warded Arms

passive
Gain a weapon specialty. Your attacks deal +1 damage against a quarry that has used a power within the last minute.

Collected

passive
+1d to resist Mental and Social rolls.

Read the Signs

Occult 3+
When your quarry uses a power, roll Wits + Occult, needing 2 successes, to learn which power it is, which pool it draws on, and whether that pool is below half.

Two Steps Ahead

passive · Wits 3+
While your quarry is in your reach, it can't graze you, and it spends 1 extra action to disengage from you.

Foresight

passive · Wits 4+
You get 2 reactions per round.

Reckless Ferocity

2 actions
Take 1 Stress and make two melee attacks against your quarry, the second with the usual penalty for an extra attack. This is an exception to the one-attack rule.

Extinguish

reaction · Empathy 4+
When your quarry in your reach uses a power or refills its pool, make a Reactive Strike against it. On a hit, the refill is halved and it's Stunned until the end of its next turn. A character can be extinguished this way once per minute.

Tracker

Traps, wilderness skill and an animal companion that hunts with you.

Trackerafter Seek Quarry

Deadly Trap

2 actions · Survival 2+
Conceal a trap covering a small area (see Ranges). You can have one trap set at a time, and its effects last 1 turn.
  • Entangling: Wits + Survival, contested by Dexterity + Stamina. 1 bashing damage; the target is Immobilized and the area becomes difficult terrain.
  • Impaling: Wits + Survival, contested by Dexterity + Stamina. 1 lethal damage.

Animal Bond

No prerequisite
Gain a rating 2 animal companion of your choice. While your companion is close, you get +1d to defense rolls. As a free action on your turn, give your companion 1 or 2 actions. Your companion can track (2 actions): one target near it becomes your quarry.

Experienced Trapper

Wits 3+
You can forage for as many people as your Survival dots and make natural tools with Survival. You can have two traps set at once. Your traps deal 2 damage and their effects last 2 turns.

Protective Bond

1 action
Your companion protects an ally close to you. That ally gets the Animal Bond bonus instead of you.

Surefooted

passive
+3 Speed. Reduce damage from falls and dangerous terrain by your Athletics dots.

Feral Connection

passive · Survival 3+
Gain an Animal Ken specialty. Your companion gains +3 Health and +1d to its defense rolls, and extra dice on its rolls equal to half your Survival, rounded up.

Hunter's Edge

passive · Survival 4+
Your companion gets +1d against your quarry. Your traps deal 3 damage and their effects last 3 turns.

Pack Hunting

reaction · Wits 4+
Take 1 Stress to give an ally +2d on their attack against your quarry.
