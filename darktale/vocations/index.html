PrimerRunetech
VocationRogue Rogues live by their wits and their luck: investigators, spies and thieves who turn risk into opportunity. Their key talent lets them reroll the stakes die, so they thrive when the stakes are high. Key talent: Opportunist Trees: Investigator · Spy · Thief VocationCounselor Counselors inspire, steady and protect the people around them, and turn words into weapons against their enemies. Their key talent makes allies Inspired, so the whole party fails less often. Key talent: Rousing Presence Trees: Diplomat · Faithful · Grifter · Mentor VocationHunter Hunters track, study and bring down a chosen quarry. Patient and precise, they're as comfortable in the wild as in a crowded street, as long as they know who they're after. Key talent: Seek Quarry Trees: Sharpshooter · Assassin · Magekiller · Tracker VocationLeader Leaders make everyone around them better. Their command dice add to their allies' rolls, and their talents give the party extra actions, protection and direction. Key talent: Decisive Command Trees: Mastermind · Officer · Paragon · Politico VocationScholar Scholars treat knowledge as a tool. Their key talent, Erudition, gives them expertise they can reassign after a rest with access to research, so they can prepare for whatever comes next. Key talent: Erudition Trees: Engineer · Strategist · Surgeon VocationWarrior Masters of weapons and fighting stances, warriors are at their best in the thick of a fight, blade in hand, fists up or gun drawn. They hold the line, trade blows with the toughest enemies, and make it costly for anyone to get past them. Key talent: Vigilant Stance Trees: Brawler · Duelist · Gunslinger · Juggernaut · Soldier Vocation · DuskworldAthrenite Athrenites are humans who carry a power that isn't theirs, and know it by their orange blood. SPEAR infuses its chosen with it in sealed laboratories, so that the power that once served the Allmother's murderers now serves Mendris. Some say it came from the First Followers' blasphemy; some say the Allmother allows it, others that she curses it. No one knows, and few agree. The power is called Athren, and each athrenite belongs to one Form of it. The power is fast and strong, and it's always hungry: use it hard enough and it consumes you, and you become a Transcendent. Key talent: Orange Blood Trees: Guardian · Harbinger · Listener · Mawbound · Shardbound · Veilwalker · Vessel Vocation · DuskworldDreamshaper Some kehars carry the blood of the First Dreamers and can reach into the Everdream, the dream that dreamed the world, to make their own dreams real. Their gift is Dreamshaping, and the effects they create are called dreamcrafts. Key talent: Dreamer's Blood Trees: Mesmer · Maker Vocation · DuskworldShadowcaller Shanats are the children of Rakshan, the Shadow Hunter, and some carry enough of his blood to call on his gifts: the powers of a hunter of the night. Their gift is Shadowcalling, and its powers are called blessings. The stronger the blessing, the louder the forefather whispers. Key talent: Forefather's Blessing Trees: Ashtai · Kalestri · Meneren · Vash'arai · Velkhar
