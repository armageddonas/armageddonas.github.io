Vocation

Envoy

Envoys inspire, steady and protect the people around them, and turn words into weapons against their enemies. Their key talent makes allies Inspired, so the whole party fails less often.

Vocation skills
Persuasion, Expression or Empathy
Starting gear
One Light weapon, light armor or none, and a writing case.

Playing an envoy

In combat

You support. You inspire allies, help them recover Stress and shake off conditions, and challenge enemies to throw them off balance or calm them down.

In social encounters

You're the party's voice. You persuade, give speeches and move in high society.

While exploring

You keep the group going. Your talents clear Stress on long journeys, and you're a natural helper on teamwork rolls.

Key talent1 action

Rousing Presence

Take 1 Stress. An ally near you becomes Inspired for one hour.

Diplomat

Challenging and calming enemies, and moving in high society.

Diplomatafter Rousing Presence

Steadfast Challenge

1 action · Resolve 2+
Take 1 Stress and roll Resolve + Intimidation, contested by Wits + Composure. On a success, the enemy is Disoriented until the end of their next turn, and has −1d on rolls against you for a number of rounds equal to your successes.

Collected

passive
+1d to resist Mental and Social rolls.

Withering Retort

Resolve 3+
When you react to an attack, you can use Steadfast Challenge as a free action. If you succeed, you get +2d on your reaction, but the attacker doesn't suffer Steadfast Challenge's other effects.

Well Dressed

passive
Gain a Socialize (Fashion) specialty. While dressed for the occasion, +1d on your first Subterfuge, Expression or Persuasion roll each hour.

Calm Appeal

Resolve 3+
When Steadfast Challenge leaves a target Disoriented, take 1 Stress to calm them: for as long as the challenge's −1d lasts, they also take −1d on all attacks and hostile actions, not only against you. A target who wins the contest against your Steadfast Challenge still takes 1 Stress.

High Society Contacts

patron in high society
Gain a Socialize (High Society) specialty. Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a roll to interact in high society.

Peaceful Solution

passive · Resolve 4+
Attacks made by enemies you've calmed against allies close to you take a further −1d.

Practiced Oratory

Persuasion 4+
When you use Rousing Presence or Steadfast Challenge, take Stress up to your Persuasion dots. Each point adds one more target. For Steadfast Challenge, make one roll; each target contests it separately.

Faithfulafter Rousing Presence
