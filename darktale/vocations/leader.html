Vocation

Leader

Leaders make everyone around them better. Their command dice add to their allies' rolls, and their talents give the party extra actions, protection and direction.

Vocation skills
Expression, Persuasion or IntimidationAt character creation, put 1 skill dot in one of them.
Starting gear
One weapon plus a small shield or a second weapon, light or medium armor, and a writing case.

Playing a leader

In combat

You coordinate. You hand out command dice, let allies act out of turn, and protect or position them where they're needed.

In social encounters

You command respect. You persuade, intimidate or scheme, and you understand how power works.

While exploring

You organize. You requisition supplies, keep the group moving and make sure everyone pulls their weight.

In Duskworld

Leaders in Mendris

  • A captain of a Frontier security force. Leader (Officer or Paragon)
  • A caravan master. Leader (Officer)
  • A Great House steward. Leader (Mastermind or Politico)
  • A Sixth House cell leader. Leader (Mastermind)
  • A Shaun of the Tons running a corner of the Wastes. Leader (Politico)
  • A councillor's fixer in the Midlands. Leader (Politico)

Two vocations together:

  • A Syndicate lieutenant, or the head of a smuggling ring. Leader (Politico) + Rogue (Thief)
Key talent1 action

Decisive Command

Take 1 Stress. An ally close to you adds your command dice to their next roll.

Command dice. The base is +1d. Talents can add more, up to +4d.

Mastermind

Plans laid in advance that pay off at the right moment, and an answer for everything.

Mastermindafter Decisive Command

Master Plan

2 actions · Investigation 2+
Once per day, reveal a plan you set up off-screen: put up a boon event track of 3 boxes, plus up to your Investigation dots more for a bigger payoff. Agree with the GM what the plan achieves when the track fills: a 3-box plan buys about one favor's worth (a guard shift bribed away, a door left unlocked, a rival's ally lured off); 5 boxes or more can remove an obstacle outright. You and allies near you can spend Opportunities on it. It lapses at your next rest, and you can have one plan at a time.

Composed

passive
+2 maximum Stress.

Stick to the Plan

reaction
When your stakes die or the stakes die of an ally near you shows 3–4, take 2 Stress to fill a box of your Master Plan.

Behind You!

reaction · Expression 3+
When an ally near you is attacked, they can Dodge or Endure using your reaction instead of their own. You pay any Stress it costs.

On My Signal

Investigation 3+
When your Master Plan fills, you can hold it instead of triggering it at once, and trigger it after any roll or at the end of any turn within the next hour.

Precise Command

Expression 3+
+1d to your command dice. Take 1 Stress to add your command dice to your own Investigation, Academics or Expression roll.

Smooth Execution

Expression 4+
When your Master Plan triggers, you and allies near you become Determined.

I've Got Just the Thing

Investigation 4+
Once per hour, take 1 Stress to produce an item you could have bought or obtained earlier. Pay its price and carry its bulk, as if you'd had it all along.

Officer

Battlefield command, logistics and coordinated assaults.

Officerafter Decisive Command

Composed

passive
+2 maximum Stress.

Through the Fray

1 action · Persuasion 2+
An ally close to you can disengage or take a set-up action as a reaction.

Well Supplied

Persuasion 3+
Gain a Politics (Military Logistics) specialty. Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a roll to requisition resources.

Customary Garb

passive
While dressed appropriately, +1d to Social rolls and to resist Social rolls.

Relentless March

passive · Persuasion 4+
Decisive Command also gives the target +3 Speed. Until their next turn, they ignore Exhausted, Slowed and Surprised.

Confident Command

Expression 3+
+1d to your command dice. Take 1 Stress to add your command dice to your own Intimidation, Expression or Persuasion roll.

Synchronized Assault

3 actions · Expression 4+
Roll Presence + Expression, contested by the target's Intelligence + Resolve. On a success, choose allies up to your Expression dots and take 1 Stress for each; on their next turns, each gains an extra action for one more attack against the target (see Extra attacks). This is an exception to the one-attack rule. On a failure, only one ally gains it, for 1 Stress.

Authority

rank commanding 5+ people
Double the range of your Leader talents that affect allies, and the number of allies they affect.

Paragon

Leading from the front, stepping in to protect allies and refusing to fall.

Paragonafter Decisive Command

Combat Coordination

free action · Expression 3+
Once per turn, after you attack, use Decisive Command without spending an action. If the attack missed, it also costs no Stress.

Valiant Intervention

1 action · Athletics 2+
Take 1 Stress, move up to half your Speed, then roll Presence + Athletics against an enemy in your reach, contested by its Wits + Composure. On a success, it gets −1d on rolls against your allies until the end of its next turn.

Imposing Posture

passive · Athletics 3+
When an enemy within your weapon's reach wins a contested Social roll against you, they still become Disoriented until the end of their next turn.

Hardy

passive
+2 Health.

Mighty

passive
When you hit with a weapon or unarmed attack, deal +1 damage per action spent on the attack.

Resolute Stand

Athletics 2+
Valiant Intervention doesn't provoke Reactive Strikes. Take Stress up to your Expression dots to add that many targets to it.

Resilient Hero

reaction · Athletics 4+
Once per day, when damage would fill your last Health box, instead leave empty boxes equal to your Athletics dots.

Demonstrative Command

Expression 3+
+1d to your command dice. Take 1 Stress to add your command dice to your own Athletics or Expression roll, or to any roll using Dexterity.

Politico

Schemes, rumors and deception. Turns enemies against each other.

Politicoafter Decisive Command

Cutthroat Tactics

Subterfuge 2+
An ally can raise the stakes instead of using your command dice. If the stakes die shows a Complication, you clear 1 Stress.

Tactical Ploy

1 action
Take 1 Stress and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge, contested by Intelligence + Resolve. On a success, the target loses their reaction and gets −1d on their next Mental or Social roll.

Rumormonger

requires a patron
Gain a Politics (Scandal) specialty. Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a roll to spread misinformation or gather rumors.

Well Dressed

passive
Gain a Socialize (Fashion) specialty. While dressed for the occasion, +1d on your first Subterfuge, Expression or Persuasion roll each hour.

Baleful

passive
When you win a contested Mental or Social roll against a target, they also take 1 Stress.

Set at Odds

2 actions · Expression 4+
Take Stress equal to the number of targets. Roll Presence + Expression, contested by the highest Wits + Composure among them. On a success, until the end of their next turn, they're Disoriented, can't use teamwork or aid each other, and take −1d on rolls that would help one another. Outside a fight, they distrust each other for one hour instead.

Shrewd Command

Expression 3+
+1d to your command dice. Take 1 Stress to add your command dice to your own Subterfuge, Empathy or Expression roll.

Grand Deception

3 actions · Subterfuge 4+
Take 3 Stress and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge, needing 3 successes. On a success, reveal a ruse you set up earlier that changes one detail of the situation.
