Shadowcaller

Shanats are the children of Rakshan, the Shadow Hunter, and some carry enough of his blood to call on his gifts: the powers of a hunter of the night. Their gift is Shadowcalling, and its powers are called blessings. The stronger the blessing, the louder the forefather whispers.

Playing a shadowcaller

In combat

You armor yourself in hardened darkness, grow claws, pin your enemies' shadows, drain their strength, or take the shape of a beast of the night.

In social encounters

Shadows lend weight to your words, plant desires in other minds, and at your strongest let you ride inside another's body.

While exploring

You see in the dark, step from one shadow to another, speak through shadows across any distance, and watch your targets from afar.

Key talent1 action

Forefather's Blessing

Gain the Shadowcalling power skill and an Umbra pool, and choose your first bloodline. Fangs: you can grow fangs at will; your bite is an unarmed attack dealing 1 lethal damage. When you bite a creature that you're grappling, or one that's Unconscious or willing, you can spend 1 action to drain its blood: it gains 1 stack of Exhausted, and you either regain 2 Umbra or heal 1 lethal damage (or 2 bashing).

Shanats only. Shadowcaller can't be your first vocation: take its key talent with the free pick at character creation, or through advancement. The key talent gives the Shadowcalling power skill and an Umbra pool. Using shanat powers is forbidden in Mendris.

Shadowcalling

  • Power skill: Shadowcalling. Each blessing names the attribute it's rolled with.
  • Umbra: the pool is 2 + the higher of Resolve or Presence. It refills at the end of a rest and at the end of a week of downtime, and drinking blood, with your Fangs or from a redvial, refills it too. When it's empty, you can pay with 2 Stress per point, like any power pool.
  • Always a gamble: every blessing roll raises the stakes.
  • Calling a blessing takes actions equal to its Umbra cost.
  • Duration: a blessing lasts one minute per success unless it says otherwise. You can sustain it for 1 Umbra per extra unit; its initial cost stays reserved while it lasts.

The forefather's price

The roll decides what the blessing costs.

ResultEffectUmbra cost
Success with OpportunityWorksNone
SuccessWorksFull
FailureDoesn't workNone
Failure with ComplicationDoesn't work1, and Rakshan's whispers press in: take 1 Stress

A Complication on a successful blessing is an ordinary setback, chosen by the GM.

  • Light: shadowcalling is hardest in the light. Blessing rolls take −3d in bright light (direct daylight, a Beacon's glare) and −1d in ordinary light (indoors by day, a cloudy sky). Dim light and darkness carry no penalty.
  • Forefather's Whispers: when you resist an effect aimed at krens, take −1d per two Shadowcalling dots, rounded up.

Bloodlines

Each bloodline is a talent tree. The bloodlines were separate once, in the First Children, but the years have muddled them, and few shanats keep to one.

  • Your first bloodline comes with the key talent. Its blessings are ordinary talents.
  • Opening another bloodline means taking its first blessing. It costs a talent and either the blood price (spend one blood price) or the bloodless way (also spend one choice from the advancement pool). After that, its blessings are ordinary talents too.
  • The blood price: you gain one when you kill a krenblooded creature by draining it with your Fangs and give up the drain's benefit, or when you drink a kren redvial.
  • Redvials: sealed vials of blood, the civilized way for a shanat to drink. Drinking one takes 1 action and gives the same benefit as draining with your Fangs. A kren redvial holds a krenblooded creature's dying blood instead: rare and illegal, and drinking it pays one blood price rather than giving the drain's benefit.
  • Pure or mixed: a shadowcaller who keeps to one bloodline reaches its strongest blessings sooner; one who mixes them has more tools. Both are true to Rakshan's blood.

Ashtai

The Watcher. The eyes and ears of the night: hiding, scrying, and becoming the night itself.

Ashtaiafter Forefather's Blessing

Listening Shadows

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Wits + Shadowcalling and choose a willing creature within 10 meters. While it lasts, you can speak mind to mind through your shadows at any distance, as long as both of you cast a shadow or stand in darkness. The speaker's shadow takes on the other's shape, and the voice carries no accent or other telltale sign.

Shadowcloak

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Dexterity + Shadowcalling. While you move no more than one Move a turn, you get +1d to Stealth, and rolls to spot you take −1d.

Umbral Sight

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Wits + Shadowcalling. You ignore penalties from darkness within 10 meters per Shadowcalling dot, even total darkness and the deep Dusklands.

Eyes of the Night

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra. Roll Intelligence + Shadowcalling and choose someone within 10 kilometers per Shadowcalling dot. You see and hear what happens within a few meters of them, blurred: −2d to rolls to notice details. An unwilling target contests with Wits + Composure. You get +1d if you hold a piece of them (hair, blood) or something of great meaning to them, and −1d if you only know of them. It fails on the dead and on targets warded by stronger magic, and magic that reveals hidden effects can detect it.

Shadowstep

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra, instant. Roll Dexterity + Shadowcalling. Step into a shadow large and dark enough to hold you, and out of another within 10 meters. For 4 Umbra, you can do it as a reaction. Until the end of your turn, defense rolls against your attacks take −2d.

After Shadowcloak or Umbral Sight

Also available after Umbral Sight

Nightform

3 actions · Shadowcalling 4+
3 Umbra. Roll Resolve + Shadowcalling. You become the night itself. While it lasts, you're Obscured, attacks against you take −2d, you make no sound, and you pass through any gap a shadow could. You can't attack, cast other blessings or hold anything. In bright light it ends at once, and you're Stunned until the end of your next turn.

After Eyes of the Night or Shadowstep

Also available after Shadowstep

Nightform listed above ↑Shadowstep listed above ↑

The eyes and ears of the night. Ashtai blessings mostly roll Wits, Dexterity or Intelligence.

Kalestri

The Whisperer. Deceit, illusion and mastery over other minds.

Kalestriafter Forefather's Blessing

False Reflection

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Manipulation + Shadowcalling. In at least dim light, a shadow double mimics your movements, slightly wrong. Rolls to read your actions or to react to them, including defense rolls against your attacks, take −2d, or −1d for anyone adjacent to you. When such a roll fails, you choose what they mistook your action for.

Glibness of Shadow

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Presence + Shadowcalling. In at least dim light, you get +1d to Social rolls to influence others, and rolls to read your emotions or catch your lies take −1d.

Umbral Temptation

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Manipulation + Shadowcalling, contested by Intelligence + Resolve, against someone within 10 meters. On a success, you plant a powerful but wholly artificial desire: adoration of you, hatred of a friend, hunger for something they never wanted. They act on it as if it were their own while it lasts, following the compulsion rules.

Shadow Possession

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra. Touch a creature's shadow and roll Manipulation + Shadowcalling, contested by Intelligence + Resolve. On a success, you melt into its shadow: your body vanishes, and you share its senses. You can give it one command at a time, following the compulsion rules. Bright light on the creature ends it and casts you out beside it.

After Umbral Temptation or Glibness of Shadow

Also available after Glibness of Shadow

Shadowplay

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra. Roll Manipulation + Shadowcalling. The shadows over an area or a creature within 10 meters take on a false appearance: a dog looks like a monster, sounds like it roars, feels like silk. You change one sense, plus one more per two Shadowcalling dots, and can't change it again once set. Anyone who interacts with it contests with Wits + Composure to shrug off its effects (pain, fear, nausea), but it can't harm anyone. Bright light dispels it.

Form of the Whisperer

3 actions · Shadowcalling 4+
3 Umbra. Roll Presence + Shadowcalling. The shadows around you rise and become real, showing the secrets and fears of those nearby. While it lasts, you gain +1 Dexterity, your unarmed attacks are claws dealing 1 lethal, and enemies within 10 meters take −1d on rolls against you. In total darkness, non-magical damage against you is halved, rounded down. Once per turn, for 1 action, a shadow within 20 meters (in at least dim light) rises and takes one action of yours with your dice, then sinks back. If it attacks, that's your attack for the turn.

After Shadowplay or Shadow Possession

Also available after Shadow Possession

Shadow Possession listed above ↑Form of the Whisperer listed above ↑

Deceit, illusion and other minds. Kalestri blessings mostly roll Manipulation or Presence.

Meneren

The Formless. Changing shape, from swifter limbs to the forms of creatures of the night.

Menerenafter Forefather's Blessing

Umbral Sight

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Wits + Shadowcalling. You ignore penalties from darkness within 10 meters per Shadowcalling dot, even total darkness and the deep Dusklands.

Umbral Speed

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Dexterity + Shadowcalling. Your Speed increases by 2 per Shadowcalling dot.

Shadow Limbs

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Dexterity + Shadowcalling. Your limbs stretch and bend like shadows: your unarmed attacks gain Reach, and you get +1d to climb and to grapple.

Form of the Hunter

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra, lasting ten minutes per success. Roll Stamina + Shadowcalling. You take the shape of a predator beast made of wisps of shadow, built to run down prey and kill it. You gain +1 Strength and +1 Dexterity, and your bite and claws are unarmed attacks dealing 1 lethal. Choose one: Leaper (after a Move of at least 3 meters, +1d to your next attack this turn), Many-legged (+4 Speed, +2d to resist being knocked Prone) or Flying (you fly at your Speed + 2). In this form you can't speak or use weapons and tools; if you're knocked out, you return to your own shape.

After Umbral Speed or Shadow Limbs

Also available after Shadow Limbs

Form of the Stalker

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra, lasting ten minutes per success. Roll Dexterity + Shadowcalling. You take the shape of an insect the size of a child, made of wisps of shadow, built to creep, spy and get in where no one else can. You gain +1 Dexterity. You move without a sound, climb any surface at your full Speed without a roll, get +2d to Stealth, and slip through any gap a child could. Your bite is an unarmed attack dealing 1 lethal; against an unsuspecting target, it also leaves them Stunned until the end of their next turn. Choose one: Many-legged (+4 Speed, +2d to resist being knocked Prone) or Flying (you fly at your Speed + 2). In this form you can't speak or use weapons and tools; if you're knocked out, you return to your own shape.

Form of the Formless

3 actions · Shadowcalling 4+
3 Umbra. Roll Resolve + Shadowcalling. You merge the Hunter and the Stalker into the Formless, a terrifying creature of smoke, silence and entropy. While it lasts, you gain +1 Strength and +1 Dexterity, your claws are unarmed attacks dealing 1 lethal, you fly at your Speed, attacks against you take −1d as your shape flickers, and you slip through narrow gaps and under doors without a roll. In total darkness, non-magical damage against you is halved, rounded down.

After Form of the Hunter or Form of the Stalker

Also available after Form of the Stalker

Form of the Formless listed above ↑Form of the Hunter listed above ↑

Changing shape. When you call a Form blessing, any other Form blessing you have active ends. Meneren blessings mostly roll Dexterity, Stamina or Resolve.

Vash'arai

The Beast. The resilience of darkness, and the strength to withstand and deliver terrible blows.

Vash'araiafter Forefather's Blessing

Umbral Armor

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Stamina + Shadowcalling. Darkness hardens around you: +1 armor rating, or +2 at Shadowcalling 4+, on top of anything you wear. It has no Wear track, and it vanishes while you're in bright light.

Umbral Fortitude

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Stamina + Shadowcalling. Darkness steadies your flesh: you gain 2 extra Health boxes while it lasts (damage in them is lost when it ends), +2d to Endure rolls, and in dim light or darkness, being Beaten Down doesn't make your attacks cost Stress. You can call it as a reaction, but then it lasts only for the roll it was called for, and gives no extra Health.

Umbral Strength

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Strength + Shadowcalling. Your melee and unarmed attacks deal +1 damage, or +2 at Shadowcalling 4+.

Shadow Mend

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra. Roll Stamina + Shadowcalling. Shadows knit your flesh. While it lasts, you stop Bleeding and can't start, and you heal 1 bashing damage at the end of each minute. When it ends, you heal 1 lethal damage.

After Umbral Fortitude or Umbral Armor

Also available after Umbral Armor

Shadow Strike

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra. Roll Strength + Shadowcalling. Claws of darkness grow from your arms: your unarmed attacks deal 1 lethal and ignore half of any non-magical armor rating, rounded up.

Form of the Beast

3 actions · Shadowcalling 4+
3 Umbra. Roll Strength + Shadowcalling. Shadows coil around you and harden into the Form of the Beast. While it lasts, you gain +1 Strength, +1 Dexterity and +1 Stamina, +1d to Intimidation, and claws dealing 1 lethal; non-magical damage against you is halved, rounded down. For 1 action, you can roar: everyone within 10 meters contests your Presence + Intimidation with Wits + Composure or becomes Frightened of you while the blessing lasts. On the turn you call the blessing, the roar is a free action and uses your Shadowcalling roll.

After Shadow Strike or Shadow Mend

Also available after Shadow Mend

Shadow Mend listed above ↑Form of the Beast listed above ↑

The resilience of darkness. Vash'arai blessings mostly roll Strength or Stamina.

Velkhar

The Devourer. Drawing warmth, vitality and strength from the living, weakening foes to feed yourself.

Velkharafter Forefather's Blessing

Shadow Trap

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Wits + Shadowcalling, contested by Dexterity + Stamina, against a creature within 10 meters that casts a shadow. On a success, you pin its shadow to the ground: it's Immobilized while the blessing lasts. Breaking free takes 1 action and Strength + Athletics against your roll, and ends the blessing. It ends at once if the creature is in total darkness.

Sense Warmth

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Wits + Shadowcalling. You sense every living creature within 10 meters per Shadowcalling dot by its warmth, even through walls and in total darkness, and you know roughly how hurt each one is.

Shadow Veil

1 action
1 Umbra. Roll Resolve + Shadowcalling. Within 10 meters, the light drops one step: bright light becomes ordinary, ordinary becomes dim, dim becomes darkness. Candles snuff out, and each lantern goes out on a roll of 1–3 on a die. It can't dim the Allmother's light.

Umbral Drain

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra. Roll Presence + Shadowcalling, contested by Dexterity + Stamina, against up to one creature per Shadowcalling dot within 10 meters. Choose Strength, Dexterity or Stamina: each creature you beat loses 1 dot of it (to a minimum of 1) while the blessing lasts, and you gain 1 dot of it. Victims of a drained Stamina look wasted and skeletal.

Cold Touch

2 actions · Shadowcalling 3+
2 Umbra, instant. Touch a creature and roll Presence + Shadowcalling, contested by Stamina + Resolve. On a success, you draw the warmth out of it: it gains 1 stack of Exhausted, and you either regain 1 Umbra or clear 2 Stress.

After Shadow Veil or Sense Warmth

Also available after Sense Warmth

Devour

3 actions · Shadowcalling 4+
3 Umbra. Roll Presence + Shadowcalling, contested by Stamina + Resolve, against up to one creature per Shadowcalling dot within 10 meters. Shadow tendrils tether each one you beat to you. While it lasts, at the end of each of your turns, each tethered creature takes 1 bashing damage, and for each one you heal 1 bashing damage, or 1 lethal damage if that creature's damage became lethal (because its Health was already full). A tether breaks if its creature gets more than 20 meters away or steps into bright light.

After Umbral Drain or Cold Touch

Also available after Cold Touch

Cold Touch listed above ↑Devour listed above ↑

Feeding on the living. Velkhar blessings mostly roll Wits, Resolve or Presence.
