Vocation

Warrior

Masters of weapons and fighting stances, warriors are at their best in the thick of a fight, blade in hand, fists up or gun drawn. They hold the line, trade blows with the toughest enemies, and make it costly for anyone to get past them.

Vocation skills
Weaponry, Brawl or AthleticsAt character creation, put 1 skill dot in one of them.
Starting gear
Two weapons, or one weapon and a shield, and medium armor (heavy armor and a heavy weapon if you take Heavy Training). No kit.

Playing a warrior

In combat

You stand at the front and take the hits meant for others. Vigilant Stance makes your parries and Reactive Strikes cheap, and switching stances lets you change role mid-fight.

In social encounters

You're an imposing presence. Your stances and training help with Intimidation, and you're at home in military circles.

While exploring

You're strong and hard to catch off guard. You carry the heavy gear, cross rough ground, and keep watch.

In Duskworld

Warriors in Mendris

  • A soldier of the Frontier security forces. Warrior (Soldier)
  • A House guard licensed to carry a lock pistol. Warrior (Gunslinger)
  • A fighter at the Gutterlord's boxing nights. Warrior (Brawler)
  • A Gentry duellist. Warrior (Duelist)
  • A Southbank dock enforcer. Warrior (Brawler or Juggernaut)

Two vocations together:

  • A hunter of the Dusktouched beyond the Beacons, who sees as well at night as by day. Warrior (Soldier or Juggernaut) + Shadowcaller (Vash'arai)
Key talent1 action

Vigilant Stance

Enter Vigilant Stance. While in it, your first extra reaction each round costs 1 Stress instead of 2 when used to Parry or make a Reactive Strike, and you can enter other stances as a free action.

Brawler

Unarmed and improvised fighting in a crowd: grapples, counterpunches and blows that land where it hurts.

Brawlerafter Vigilant Stance

Grappler's Stance

1 action · Intimidation 2+
Enter Grappler's Stance: +1d to Grapple rolls and to Athletics rolls that aren't attacks. Enemies in your reach spend 1 extra action to draw a weapon or make a ranged attack (this doesn't add up with other grapplers).

Baleful

passive
When you win a contested Mental or Social roll against a target, they also take 1 Stress.

Surefooted

passive
+3 Speed. Reduce damage from falls and dangerous terrain by your Athletics dots.

Bruiser's Stance

1 action · Intimidation 3+
Enter Bruiser's Stance: +1d to Dodge and Parry for each enemy in your reach beyond the first, up to +2d. Your unarmed and improvised Reactive Strikes don't use your reaction: each costs 1 Stress instead, once per enemy per round.

Roll With the Punches

reaction · Athletics 3+
When you're hit or grazed, move up to half your Speed without provoking Reactive Strikes.

Pulse of Battle

passive
Once per round, when you're hit or grazed, you become Determined.

Battle-Hardened

passive · Athletics 4+
Your Wounded threshold is 1 higher, which also raises your Exhausted collapse point. Your unarmed and improvised attacks deal +1 damage.

Wallop

reaction · Intimidation 4+
When an enemy close to you misses or grazes you, take 2 Stress, move up to half your Speed and make an unarmed or improvised attack against them. It deals +2 damage and ignores armor rating. On an Opportunity, choose one: they're Disoriented until the end of their next turn, they drop what they're holding, or they fall Prone.

Duelist

A one-on-one fighter who flows between stances and wears enemies down with feints and pressure.

Duelistafter Vigilant Stance

Drilled Forms

No prerequisite
You know subtle ways to use your stances outside of combat, and gain their bonuses there too. Unless Surprised or already in a stance, you start every fight in Vigilant Stance.

Twin Weapon Stance

1 action · Dexterity 3+
Enter Twin Weapon Stance: +1d to Dexterity rolls that aren't attacks. While you hold a one-handed weapon in each hand, your attacks get an Opportunity on 4+ successes (this doesn't stack with Signature Weapon or other talents that expand the Opportunity range), and your off-hand attack can use any one-handed weapon instead of only a Light one, for 2 Stress instead of 1.

Challenger's Stance

1 action · Intimidation 2+
Enter Challenger's Stance: +1d to Intimidation. When only one enemy is in your reach and no allies are, you can either attack or take a set-up action without spending an action. The one-attack rule still applies.

Counter Stance

1 action · Athletics 3+
Enter Counter Stance: +1d to Empathy. When an attacker misses or only grazes you, you can make a Reactive Strike against them.

Signature Weapon

passive
Gain a weapon specialty. Choose one of your weapon specialties: attacks with that weapon get an Opportunity on 4+ successes. If it's a Light weapon, you can attack with Dexterity instead of Strength.

Feinting Strike

1 action · Intimidation 3+
Take 2 Stress and make a melee weapon attack, contested by Intelligence + Resolve. On a hit, the target loses their reaction and takes Stress equal to your net successes, up to your Intimidation. On a graze, they take 1 Stress and keep their reaction.

Flowing Stance

1 action · Wits 3+
Enter Flowing Stance: +1d to rolls using Dexterity. While multiple enemies are in your reach, you can either disengage or attack without spending an action. The one-attack rule still applies.

Breaking Point

2 actions · Intimidation 4+
Take 1 Stress, move up to half your Speed, then make a melee weapon attack against a target with a full Stress track. The attack ignores armor, deals +3 damage and can't graze.

Gunslinger

Firearms up close: trick shots, quick reloads and fanning a pistol across a room.

Gunslingerafter Vigilant Stance

Fire Discipline

passive
Gain Ranged (Firearms) and Crafts (Firearms) specialties. Once per round, you can graze with a firearm without taking Stress.

Trick Shooting

Dexterity 2+
After a successful Dexterity or Wits set-up, your attacks with one-handed firearms ignore the target's cover until the end of your turn, and full cover counts as ordinary cover: you bank the shot off a wall or a lamp.

Twin Weapon Stance

1 action · Dexterity 3+
Enter Twin Weapon Stance: +1d to Dexterity rolls that aren't attacks. While you hold a one-handed weapon in each hand, your attacks get an Opportunity on 4+ successes (this doesn't stack with Signature Weapon or other talents that expand the Opportunity range), and your off-hand attack can use any one-handed weapon instead of only a Light one, for 2 Stress instead of 1.

Terrifying Jolt

Intimidation 3+
When you graze with a firearm, the target also takes 1 Stress. A minion, or anyone with a full Stress track, is instead Frightened of you until the end of their next turn.

Mighty

passive
When you hit with a weapon or unarmed attack, deal +1 damage per action spent on the attack.

Fast Hands

Dexterity 4+
Your firearms count as Quickdraw. Once per round, take 2 Stress to reload a firearm or clear a jam without spending an action. On a fast turn, +1d to your first attack against a character who hasn't acted yet this round.

Fanning

2 actions · Dexterity 4+
With a one-handed firearm and your other hand free, attack chosen targets in a small area, up to your Dexterity and the shots loaded, at −1d for each target beyond 2. Each target spends a shot and defends separately. The one you beat by the most is hit; the others you beat are grazed, without Stress. This counts as one attack.

Collateral Damage

Wits 4+
Once per round, when you hit with a firearm, a character adjacent to the target also takes a graze.

Juggernaut

Heavy armor and heavy weapons. Shrugs off blows, breaks armor and scatters crowds.

Juggernautafter Vigilant Stance

Heavy Training

requires Heavy armor and a Heavy weapon
Gain an Athletics (Heavy Armor) specialty and a weapon specialty in one heavy weapon. Your armor's Wear track has 2 extra boxes. When you attack with the heavy weapon, extra targets in reach, up to your weapon skill dots, also take 1 bashing damage, as a graze without Stress.

Bulwark Stance

1 action
Enter Bulwark Stance: +1 armor rating. Enemies in your reach must spend 1 extra action to attack an ally who isn't in Bulwark Stance.

Repelling Stance

1 action · Athletics 3+
Enter Repelling Stance: +1d to Parry and +1d to resist Mental rolls. After you're hit or grazed in melee, use your reaction to roll Strength + Athletics, contested by Dexterity + Stamina. On a success, the attacker takes Stress equal to your net successes, up to your Athletics, and is pushed back 1 meter.

Mighty

passive
When you hit with a weapon or unarmed attack, deal +1 damage per action spent on the attack.

Shattering Blow

Wits 3+
When you hit with a melee attack, take 2 Stress. The target's armor takes 1 Wear (or loses 1 rating for one hour if its Wear track is already full), and every target you damage is pushed back a number of meters equal to the damage it took, of any type (bashing, lethal or aggravated). Count the hit's damage once, even if it lands across several severities. A graze or a hit stopped by armor still pushes 1 meter.

Reckless Stance

1 action · Athletics 3+
Enter Reckless Stance: your attacks get an Opportunity on 4+ successes, but your Defense is 0 and you take −1d to resist Mental and Social rolls.

After Mighty or Heavy Training

Also available after Heavy Training

Shrug It Off

free action · Wits 4+
When a melee attack hits you, whether or not you tried to Parry, take Stress to ignore that much damage, up to your Weaponry dots.

Unstoppable Charge

2 actions · Athletics 4+
Take 2 Stress and move up to your Speed in a straight line. Make a heavy-weapon attack against each enemy you pass within reach; each defends separately. This is an exception to the one-attack rule. Each enemy you hit is pushed aside 1 meter or falls Prone, your choice. Reactive Strikes don't stop your movement. +1d in heavy armor.

Soldier

Disciplined and defensive. Braces behind cover, holds formations and keeps allies alive.

Soldierafter Vigilant Stance

Cautious Advance

2 actions · Resolve 2+
Move up to half your Speed, ignoring difficult terrain. Then Brace or take a set-up action without spending an action.

Combat Training

passive
Gain a weapon specialty and a Socialize (Military Life) specialty, and ignore the penalties of one armor type. Once per round, you can graze without taking Stress.

Defensive Position

passive · Athletics 3+
Your Brace gives +2d instead of +1d, and allies can Brace behind you.

Devastating Blow

2 actions · Athletics 4+
Make a melee weapon attack that deals +2 damage.

Formation Drills

passive · Resolve 3+
Allies close to you who Brace also get your Defensive Position bonus.

Hardy

passive
+2 Health.

Wary

passive · Resolve 4+
You can't be Surprised while your Stress track isn't full. When an effect forces Stress on you, take 1 less, to a minimum of 1.

Swift Strikes

1 action · Weaponry, Brawl or Ranged 3+ (the weapon's skill)
Take 2 Stress to make an extra attack with the same weapon (see Extra attacks). This is an exception to the one-attack rule.
