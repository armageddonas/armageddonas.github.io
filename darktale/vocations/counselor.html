Vocation

Counselor

Counselors inspire, steady and protect the people around them, and turn words into weapons against their enemies. Their key talent makes allies Inspired, so the whole party fails less often.

Vocation skills
Persuasion, Expression or EmpathyAt character creation, put 1 skill dot in one of them.
Starting gear
One Light weapon, light armor or none, and a writing case.
Talent trees
Diplomat · Faithful · Grifter · Mentor

Playing a counselor

In combat

You support. You inspire allies, help them recover Stress and shake off conditions, and challenge enemies to throw them off balance or calm them down.

In social encounters

You're the party's voice. You persuade, give speeches and move in high society.

While exploring

You keep the group going. Your talents clear Stress on long journeys, and you're a natural helper on teamwork rolls.

In Duskworld

Counselors in Mendris

  • A parish priest of the Allmother in the Fork. Counselor (Faithful)
  • A House Merovant negotiator. Counselor (Diplomat)
  • An advocate at the Hearing House. Counselor (Diplomat) or Leader (Politico)
  • A confidence trickster working the markets of Shurnen's Bend. Counselor (Grifter)
  • A teacher at the Caliethan University. Counselor (Mentor), often with Scholar

Two vocations together:

  • A dream-confessor who eases what people bring back from the Dusklands. Counselor (Faithful) + Dreamshaper (Mesmer)
  • A Sixth House agitator, stirring the Fork against the Great Houses. Counselor (Grifter) + Rogue (Spy)
Key talent1 action

Rousing Presence

Take 1 Stress. An ally near you becomes Inspired for one hour.

Diplomat

Challenging and calming enemies, and moving in high society.

Diplomatafter Rousing Presence

Steadfast Challenge

1 action · Resolve 2+
Take 1 Stress and roll Resolve + Intimidation, contested by Wits + Composure. On a success, the enemy is Disoriented until the end of their next turn, and has −1d on rolls against you for a number of rounds equal to your successes.

Collected

passive
+1d to resist Mental and Social rolls.

Withering Retort

Resolve 3+
When you react to an attack, you can use Steadfast Challenge as a free action. If you succeed, you get +2d on your reaction, but the attacker doesn't suffer Steadfast Challenge's other effects.

Well Dressed

passive
Gain a Socialize (Fashion) specialty. While dressed for the occasion, +1d on your first Subterfuge, Expression or Persuasion roll each hour.

Calm Appeal

Resolve 3+
When Steadfast Challenge leaves a target Disoriented, take 1 Stress to calm them: for as long as the challenge's −1d lasts, they also take −1d on all attacks and hostile actions, not only against you. A target who wins the contest against your Steadfast Challenge still takes 1 Stress.

High Society Contacts

patron in high society
Gain a Socialize (High Society) specialty. Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a roll to interact in high society.

Peaceful Solution

passive · Resolve 4+
Attacks made by enemies you've calmed against allies close to you take a further −1d.

Practiced Oratory

Persuasion 4+
When you use Rousing Presence or Steadfast Challenge, take Stress up to your Persuasion dots. Each point adds one more target. For Steadfast Challenge, make one roll; each target contests it separately.

Faithful

Devotion that shields allies and restores them.

Faithfulafter Rousing Presence

Customary Garb

passive
While dressed appropriately, +1d to Social rolls and to resist Social rolls.

Galvanize

2 actions
Once per day, an ally close to you makes a recovery roll.

Devoted Presence

Academics 2+
When you use Rousing Presence, take 1 Stress to remove Prone, Slowed, Stunned and Surprised from the target.

Composed

passive
+2 maximum Stress.

Stalwart Presence

Resolve 3+
When you use Rousing Presence, take 1 Stress to give the target +1d to defense rolls, or +1d to resist, for one hour.

Applied Motivation

passive · Academics 3+
When you use Galvanize, the ally clears 1 extra Stress.

Sage Counsel

Academics 4+
When you help a character with teamwork, take 1 Stress to also make them Inspired.

Inspired Zeal

Resolve 4+
When an ally uses Inspired, you can take 1 Stress to choose other allies, up to your Resolve dots. Each becomes Determined.

Grifter

Con artistry: flattery, absurd distractions and bluffs that leave a mark reeling.

Grifterafter Rousing Presence

False Flattery

1 action
Take 1 Stress and flatter a non-ally near you. The first time within the next hour that they fail a roll against you or an ally, you or that ally gain an Opportunity on the next roll against them.

Plausible Excuse

reaction · Subterfuge 2+
Gain a Thievery (Sleight of Hand) specialty. When you're caught skulking, take 2 Stress to feign innocence.

This One's on Me

reaction · Resolve 2+
When a ranged attack targets an ally near you, take 1 Stress and move up to half your Speed to get in the way. The attack targets you and hits (no defense roll, armor applies), and the ally becomes Inspired.

Outrageous Quip

1 action · Subterfuge 3+
Say something absurd with a straight face. Take 1 Stress and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge, contested by Wits + Composure. On a success, the target is Surprised until the start of your next turn. A character can be caught out this way once per minute.

The Long Con

passive
Gain a patron in high society or the underworld. Wherever they have influence, they give you lodging, travel and the materials for any mundane disguise, and you get +1d to disguise rolls.

Got 'Em Good!

reaction · Resolve 3+
Once per round, after an ally wins a contested Mental or Social roll against a target near you, the target takes 2 Stress. If their Stress track is full, they're Disoriented until the start of your next turn instead.

Exceptional Bluff

Subterfuge 4+
Take 2 Stress to use Subterfuge in place of another skill on a Mental or Social roll to influence someone.

Insult to Injury

Subterfuge 4+
When False Flattery gives you or an ally an Opportunity, whoever gains it can spend it to catch the flattered target off guard: their next attack against them before the end of your next turn is an ambush, and they're Surprised against it, so talents that need an unsuspecting target work on it.

Mentorafter Rousing Presence

Sound Advice

reaction
Use Rousing Presence as a reaction on an ally near you who just failed a roll. They can use Inspired on that roll right away.

Practical Demonstration

free action · Expression 2+
When you take a set-up action or hit with an attack, use Rousing Presence as a free action.

Lessons in Patience

passive · Resolve 3+
Gain an Expression (Motivational Speech) specialty. The target of your Rousing Presence also clears 1 Stress.

Mighty

passive

Instill Confidence

No prerequisite
Rousing Presence can make an ally Determined instead of Inspired.

Guiding Oration

passive · Expression 3+
After you take a set-up action against a target, an ally close to that target gets +1d on their next roll against it.

Foresight

passive · Resolve 4+

Expression 4+
