Reading a talent

Each talent is written as Name followed by tags, then its effect:

  • after: the talent you need before you can take this one. Talents tagged after key talent start a tree and need only the vocation's key talent. Where two talents are listed ("after A or B"), either one is enough.
  • Cost: 1 action, 2 actions, 3 actions, free action, reaction or passive.
  • Prerequisites: skills, attributes, community traits or gear the talent requires, such as Wits 3+ or requires an animal companion. They apply on top of the after tag.

Prerequisites count only permanent dots. Temporary dots from talents (such as the Scholar's Erudition) help your rolls but never meet a talent's or trait's prerequisite.

Talent trees

  • Each vocation has a key talent and three trees of talents.
  • You can have more than one vocation by gaining another vocation's key talent through advancement or your free pick at character creation. After that, its trees are open to you too.
  • Communities use the same tree structure for their traits (see Character Creation).

Traits

Traits are where you come from; talents are what you trained. There are three kinds in the core rules:

  • Racial traits: a race's primary and secondary trait (see Character Creation).
  • Community traits: the key and further traits of a community (see Character Creation).
  • Weapon traits: properties of a weapon, such as Deadly or Quickdraw (see Weapon traits).

Settings can add their own kinds, always named with what they belong to, such as Duskworld's Dusk traits, which come with the stages of the Dusking Scar (see The Dusk).

Terms

  • Specialties from talents and traits always name their skill, written Skill (Specialty): Socialize (Military Life), Athletics (Heavy Armor). They work even with 0 dots in the skill (see Specialties).
  • Weapon specialty: a specialty in Weaponry, Ranged or Brawl that names a weapon type, such as Weaponry (Swords), Ranged (Crossbows) or Brawl (Boxing).
  • Usage limits (once per round, minute, hour or day) are measured in in-world time (see Core Rules).
  • Unsuspecting: Surprised or unaware of you (see Ambush).
  • Quarry: one character at a time, chosen with Seek Quarry and related talents.
  • Stances: entering one takes 1 action. You're in one stance at a time; it lasts until you change it or one hour passes.
  • Patron: contacts, allies, a mentor or standing in the relevant circle, usually from a community trait that says you count as having a patron.
  • Wear: armor's damage track, used by the heavy-armor talents (see Armor).
