Houseborn

The royals of Mendris: members of one of the five Great Houses, or of the many Vassal Houses sworn to them. Most Houseborn belong to a Vassal House; a Great House name opens more doors, and draws more eyes. Their estates and townhouses fill Dawnkeep's prominent Sectors: the Midlands, seat of the Great Council, the Caliethan University and the trade of the elite, and Northbank, with its fine homes and stores.

Community skills
Politics, Persuasion or Socialize.
Starting gear
Fine clothes, full travel papers, and firearm permission: when your vocation gives you a weapon, you can take a pistol or rifle for one of them.

Community traits

Key trait

House Standing

You count as having a patron in your House, and in high society. Clerks, officials and guards treat you as someone who matters: you can get an audience, a permit or a hearing that others would wait weeks for.
Housebornafter House Standing

Family Purse

No prerequisite
You live comfortably and don't track ordinary expenses: anything that costs a handful or less costs you nothing (see Wealth). Take 2 Stress to gain an Opportunity on a roll to get something with money or influence.

Educated

Intelligence 3+
Gain an Academics or Politics specialty. When you roll an untrained Mental skill, you take −2d instead of −3d.
