Race and heritage

Your race is what you are. Your heritage is another bloodline in you: a parent of a different race, or old ancestry that has resurfaced generations later.

Each race has racial traits: a primary trait and usually a secondary trait.

  • Choose a race and take its primary trait. Then choose another or the same race as your heritage and take its secondary trait.
  • Some races have only a primary trait. Their blood is dominant: anyone with that bloodline is of that race. They can be chosen as a race but not as a heritage, so a character of that race always takes a different race as their heritage.
  • Settings add their own races, shown on this page while the setting is selected. The core rules include humans.

Racial trait trees

A race's primary trait is the root of a small tree of racial traits, read like a community's (see Talents).

  • Further racial traits come after the primary trait, each with an after tag and other prerequisites. They're who you are rather than what you trained, so they often carry a cost along with the gift.
  • Only your race's tree. Your heritage gives you its secondary trait and nothing more; it's a small bonus, not a second tree.
  • Heritage traits: some racial traits also require a particular heritage, named among their other prerequisites (Kehar heritage). A trait in the kehar tree that requires Kehar heritage is for pure-blooded kehars; one in the human tree that requires it is for a human with kehar blood.
  • Taking one: a further racial trait costs the same as a talent. Take it with your free pick at character creation, or as the talent of a milestone in advancement.
  • Secondary traits stand alone and don't start a tree.

The races

Human

Primary trait

Driven

When you push yourself, you gain +3d instead of +2d.
Humanafter Driven

All In

No prerequisite
When you push yourself, you can take 2 more Stress for +2d more, once per roll.

Strength in Numbers

No prerequisite
When allies help you with teamwork, Complications on their rolls don't carry over to yours, and their help can give you up to +3d in total instead of +2d.

Fortune's Favor

Secondary trait

You can raise the stakes on your own rolls twice per day instead of once.

In Duskworld

Human

Humans are found throughout Mendris and much of Eladria. They come in many shapes and forms, but their greatest strength is adaptability. They thrive in communities, use whatever resources are available, and have repeatedly survived circumstances that should have destroyed them.

Humans use the racial traits in the core rules (Driven and Fortune's Favor).

Only humans can carry Athren, the power of the orange-blooded: most are infused by SPEAR, a rare few are born with it (see the Athrenite).

Kehar Duskworld

Kehars are said to have been born from dreams, and a rare few can shape their dreams into reality. In a world that is steadily losing its color, kehars remain vibrant and colorful. Their appearance and culture vary between communities.

In Mendris, kehars are sometimes regarded with suspicion: some believe they're still connected to the krens because of what their ancestors did. As a result, kehars often form close-knit communities and rely heavily on one another. In the Frontiers, generations of shared hardship mean they're usually treated like their human neighbors.

Primary trait

Lucid

To a kehar, a bad outcome is only a dream that can be dreamed again. After you roll, you can reroll any number of dice that didn't succeed, taking 1 Stress per die rerolled as a single cost. You can't reroll the stakes die, or a chance die that rolled a 1.
Keharafter Lucid

Shared Dream

Empathy 2+
You can use Lucid on the roll of an ally close to you, rerolling their dice that didn't succeed. You pay the Stress, as a single cost.

Bad Dream

Composure 3+
It was only a dream. Once per day, after you roll on the Strain or Bout of Madness table, roll again and keep the result you prefer.

Dreaming Calm

Secondary trait

A part of every kehar is always dreaming, and the waking world weighs on them a little less. Once per day, you can become Determined (your next Stress cost of 2 or more is reduced by 1).

Shanat Duskworld

Shanats descend from humans and kehars touched by kren blood. Their ancestry is complicated, and most shanats don't discuss their origins openly.

The blood manifests in different ways: some shanats develop supernatural abilities tied to aspects of darkness (see the Shadowcaller), while others show only subtle physical peculiarities. Kren blood is feared in Mendris, and using its powers is forbidden, so shanats who have them use them in secret.

The Shantari, one of the Great Houses, are a shanat family. Their kren connection passed down the old codes: secret codes and keys that let them read and run kren factories and machines that remain a mystery to the rest of Mendris. This knowledge is taught, not inherited: a shanat raised outside the family doesn't have it.

Shanat blood is dominant. A child with any Shanat parent is Shanat, and the bloodline never shows up as a mere heritage. Every Shanat still carries a human or kehar bloodline, from a parent or from older ancestry resurfacing.

Shanat have only a primary trait. A Shanat character always takes a different race as their heritage, and Shanat can't be chosen as a heritage.

Primary trait

Rakshan's Blood

You have Night Sight: you ignore the dice penalty from dim light, and darkness counts as dim light for you (−1d). Total darkness and the deep Dusklands affect you as usual. Your fangs mark you as a shanat to anyone close enough to see them. Once per day, drinking a person's blood heals you 1 lethal (or 2 bashing); draining someone takes 1 action, they must be grappled, unconscious or willing, and they gain 1 Exhausted.
Shanatafter Rakshan's Blood

Blood Memory

No prerequisite
The blood remembers, and you can't refuse it. Whenever you drink a person's blood (draining them, feeding, or from a redvial), you relive the last minutes before it was taken, through their senses. On its own it's only flashes.
  • Digging deeper: take 1 Stress and roll Wits + Empathy. For each success, ask the GM one question about the memory (what they saw or heard, what they feared, who was there), and the GM answers truthfully.
  • The recently dead: you can drink from someone who died less than an hour ago, for the memory only: it gives no healing and no Umbra.
  • The curse: if the memory holds a death (they died as the blood was taken or soon after, or it's the dying blood of a kren redvial), you live it as your own: take 1 Stress, and if you dig deeper, also 1 Tenacity damage.

Blood Scent

Wits 2+
You smell blood. You know who within 10 meters is wounded or bleeding, and you get +1d to track a bleeding creature. While fresh blood is spilled near you, you take −1d to rolls using Composure.

Hidden Fangs

Composure 2+
You've learned to hide what you are. Your fangs don't mark you unless you bare them, and you get +1d to Subterfuge to pass as one of your heritage's race.
