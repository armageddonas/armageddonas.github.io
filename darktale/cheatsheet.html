Rolling

  • Pool: Attribute + Skill in d6. Each 5–6 is a success. 5+ successes is a success with Opportunity.
  • Untrained: −1d Physical or Social, −3d Mental.
  • Chance die: a pool of 0 or less rolls one die. 6 succeeds; 1 is a Complication.
  • Contested: more successes wins; ties go to the side that acts. Net successes = the margin.
  • Fixed difficulty: 2 successes is hard, 3 is very hard.
  • Teamwork: helper rolls first; 1 success gives +1d, Opportunity +2d, at most +2d in total. Their Complication carries over.
  • Group action: everyone doing the same thing rolls; the best roll counts for all. The leader takes 1 Stress per failed roll.

The GM raises the stakes when a roll could change the situation in a big way. Each player can raise them on their own roll once per day. See Rolls and results.

Turns and actions

  • Round: about 6 seconds. Order: fast PCs (2 actions), fast NPCs, slow PCs (3 actions), slow NPCs. Players choose each round.
  • One reaction per round. Take 2 Stress for an extra reaction, as many times as you can pay.
  • One attack per turn; extra attacks take a cumulative −2d.

  • Hit: your successes equal or beat the defender's. Damage = net successes + weapon damage. A tie hits for the weapon's damage alone.
  • Armor: subtract its rating; if it absorbs everything, still 1 bashing. A hit that gets past it, or an Opportunity, adds 1 Wear.

Grapple: start it with Strength + Brawl as an attack. Then, once a round, everyone in it rolls Strength + Brawl at the same time; the winner makes one move (two on an Opportunity), and a tie changes nothing.

Damage and Health

  • Health: Size + Stamina. Fills aggravated → lethal → bashing, left to right. On a full track, new damage replaces a lighter wound; if none is lighter, it worsens (bashing → lethal → aggravated) until one is.
  • Beaten Down: total damage above Stamina + Resolve. Attacking costs 1 Stress unless you can't surrender; surrendering clears 1 Stress.
  • Last box: bashing knocks you Unconscious; lethal also makes you Bleed. Stay up: take 2 Stress to stay on your feet instead (again for each further hit). Avoid Death: take a Death's Mark, stop Bleeding, stay down until healed or a rest. Blaze of Glory: one last turn where every roll counts as a success with Opportunity, then death.
  • Persistent damage lands at the end of your turn; Endure (successes ≥ rounds left) to skip it this round.

Stress and madness

  • Stress track: current Tenacity + 2. Tenacity: Resolve + Composure + 3.
  • Spend Stress: push yourself (+2d for 2), an extra reaction (2), a graze (1), an off-hand attack (1), pressing on in an extended action (1), staying up (2), breaking free (2), running a pool empty (2 per point). See Spending Stress.
  • Overflow: Stress that doesn't fit becomes Tenacity damage → madness check → clear half your Stress.
  • Madness check: after any Tenacity damage, Endure against madness: roll your remaining Tenacity in dice, needing successes equal to the damage. Fail: a Bout of Madness.
  • Bout of Madness: 3d6 − missing Tenacity (− Scar penalties). 0 or less is death; 1–4 is crippling; 5–11 is violence, panic or confusion; 12–14 Rage; 15+ is a boon.
  • Clearing Stress: 1 at each rest (+ Respite), Catch your breath, downtime, surrendering while Beaten Down.

See Stress and Tenacity and madness.

Extended actions

Each roll adds its successes. No successes: take 1 Stress to press on, or it fails. A Complication loses a success or costs time; an Opportunity saves time or materials, never adds successes. See Extended actions.

Compulsion and possession

  • Compulsion: contested; one short command; broken by harm or a self-harming order; the target can take 2 Stress to act freely for a turn but is Stunned; afterward, they know.
  • Possession: the possessor acts through the body with their own actions, paying 1 Stress per round they move it. Body's Strength, Dexterity and Stamina, possessor's everything else. Harm falls on the victim and doesn't break it. The victim can take 2 Stress on its turn to contest again.

See Compulsion.

Rest

At the end of a rest: clear 1 Stress, heal all bashing and 1 lethal, remove 1 Exhausted, refresh once-per-day abilities. Each character takes two rest actions: Recover, Prepare, Respite, Forage or Scout (2 handfuls each for extras in a settlement). See Rest.

Duskworld at the table
