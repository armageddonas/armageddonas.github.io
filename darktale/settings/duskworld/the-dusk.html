The Dusk

Rules for the Dusk's corruption: exposure, Dusking, and what happens to those it claims. For what the Dusk is, see the Overview.

Exposure

Every place has an exposure level. While characters are in it, they Endure the Dusk at the interval shown.

LevelWhereEndure everySuccesses needed
NoneBehind the Beacons, in daylight——
FaintOutside after curfew, the edges of the Beacons' reachDay1
HeavyThe Dusklands4 hours2
DeepThe deep Dusklands, duskborn lairsHour3

Enduring the Dusk: roll Resolve + Composure, needing the level's successes.

  • Success: nothing happens.
  • Failure: take Stress equal to the shortfall (the successes you were missing). It's the grey pressing on your mind. Like any Stress, it can overflow into Tenacity damage, but overflow never counts as damage from the Dusk.
  • Complication, or any failure at Deep: also take 1 Tenacity damage from the Dusk (see Dusking).

Duskborn and some kren creations can also deal Tenacity damage from the Dusk with their abilities.

Protection:

  • Grey-Weaned Dusk trait, Dusking 1 Stress from Dusk exposure is reduced by 1.
  • Hollow Calm Dusk trait once per day, a failed check costs no Stress.
  • Dusk Sense Dusklanders you know an area's exposure level before you enter it.
  • Wards and light: a wardwright's charm or a bright light source gives +1d to Endure the Dusk.

Dusking

Dusking is the Scar the Dusk leaves (see Scars). It's written on the sheet with its stage, such as Dusking 2, and never heals with rest or downtime. The Dusk takes, but it also gives: each stage brings a Dusk trait.

  • Gaining Dusking: each point of Tenacity damage from the Dusk raises Dusking by 1. Mark the new stage, gain what it brings (below), then roll the Bout of Madness.
  • Penalty to Bouts: Dusking is subtracted from every Bout of Madness roll, whatever caused it: 3d6 − missing Tenacity − Dusking.
  • The curse of the Dusk: having Dusking 1 or more is what Mendris calls the curse. The formal test detects it from stage 1.
StageEffect
1Nothing visible, but the curse test detects it. Gain the Grey-Weaned Dusk trait.
2Visible. Choose one Dusk quirk and one Dusk trait (below).

Dusk traits

Dusk traits are gifts of the curse. Once you have every trait below, the GM and player invent new ones that fit.

  • Grey-Weaned stage 1 The Dusk has less hold on you. Stress you take from Dusk exposure is reduced by 1.
  • Grey Sight: Your eyes have learned the grey. In the deep Dusklands, you see as if in darkness rather than total darkness (−3d to sight rolls instead of a chance die). Night Sight cancels that penalty.
  • Hollow Calm: Something in you has gone quiet. Once per day, when you fail to Endure the Dusk, you take no Stress from it. A Complication or a failure at Deep still deals Tenacity damage.
  • Kindred Dread: Dusktouched and duskborn creatures sense something of their own in you. You get +1d to Stealth to avoid their notice, and the first attack each of them makes against you in a fight takes −1d.
  • Unsettling: People feel the grey on you before they see it. +1d to Intimidation, but anyone who knows the signs recognizes the curse.

Dusk quirks. The GM and player choose or invent one that fits. Each is a sign of the curse and comes with a drawback:

  • Drained colour: your eyes, hair or skin turn grey. Anyone who knows the signs recognizes the curse.
  • Wrong shadow: your shadow lags or moves on its own. Animals shy away from you (−1d to Animal Ken).
  • Cold blood: you're cold to the touch and feel little warmth. −1d to Empathy rolls to comfort or connect.
  • Beacon-shy: a Beacon's light makes your skin crawl. −1d to all rolls while in a Beacon's direct light.
  • Grey dreams: you dream of the Dusk. The first time each day you clear Stress at a rest, clear 1 less.

Born under the Dusk

Characters who grew up among the Dusklanders start play with Dusking 1, and so with Grey-Weaned. They carry the curse from birth: every curse test finds it. Joining the Dusklanders later doesn't give Dusking.

Taken by the Dusk

A character can be lost to the Dusk in two ways:

  • Taken by the Dusk: a character with Dusking 1 or more who dies on a Bout of Madness. In Mendris, any death that leaves no sign of physical harm, while the victim had at least Dusking 1, is said to be taken by the Dusk.
  • Dusktouched: a character whose Dusking reaches their Resolve + Composure without dying first. They don't die. They change.

Because Dusking only rises with Tenacity damage from the Dusk, missing Tenacity and Dusking tend to rise together during a trip, so the Bout penalty effectively counts twice. Chance of death on a Bout:

Missing Tenacity + DuskingDeath chance
1 + 10%
2 + 22%
3 + 39%
4 + 426%

Tenacity heals in downtime; Dusking doesn't. A character who recovers between trips still carries their Dusking into every Bout.
