Runetech

Runetech harnesses the power of the True Runes in objects and machines, joining magic and technology. It's uncommon but not rare: few ordinary people own a piece, but everyone in Mendris lives beside it, from the Beacons and the grain silos to the railways and the firearms of the security forces. House Shantari holds runetech, railways and firearms as its purview, and its runesmiths reach the kren factories under the protection of House Valis.

Runetech items

Every runetech item holds a glint, which powers it like a Device's.

  • Runetech weapon and armor trait the item holds a glint and has the runetech abilities listed in brackets, such as Runetech (Duskward). With an empty glint, or none, the abilities don't work.
  • Using an ability: a free action that spends 1 charge from the item's glint, unless the ability says otherwise.
  • Refitting and refilling: a runetech item's glint refills and is refitted like any glint.
  • Dream phenomena and other strange forces can make runetech stop working for a while.

Runetech abilities

  • Charged firearms the weapon's mechanism runs on its glint. It spends 1 charge the first time it's fired in each minute; with no charge to spend, it can't fire.
  • Change Form: the item switches between its two forms, each with its own statistics.
  • Bind chainwhip after a hit, spend a charge as a free action. The chain coils around the target, who is Grappled by it while you hold the whip, up to 2 meters away. For 1 action, you can drag them 2 meters toward you. They break free with the Grapple action, their Strength + Brawl against your Strength + Weaponry.
  • Duskward: on armor, until the start of your next turn, its rating is doubled against attacks by dusktouched and duskborn creatures. On a weapon, until the end of the round, it has Piercing 2 against them.

Firearms

Firearms rely on intricate runetech mechanisms rather than the wielder's strength: aim, and pull the trigger. In Mendris they're legal only for the Gentry, the Houses and the security forces. They replace the core firearms and explosives (pistol, rifle, boot pistol, carbine, shotgun, grenade and dynamite), which don't exist in Duskworld and are marked as incompatible with it.

  • Jams: when a firearm attack shows a Complication, the GM can spend it to jam the weapon instead of leaving it on its last shot (see Loaded). Clearing a jam takes 1 action.
  • Ammunition isn't counted beyond the shots loaded, like other consumables, but the glint is: a firearm with no charge left is dead weight.
  • Dawnsilver rounds: a load of rounds with dawnsilver cores, SPEAR issue and rare elsewhere (1 slab on the black market). Loading them takes the reload action; until you next reload, the firearm's shots are Duskward without spending a charge.
  • Loud trait a shot is heard by everyone within a few hundred meters. Firing ends any attempt to stay hidden, and in a settlement it brings the watch.
  • Prices: a lock pistol costs 2 slabs, a Marason 3 slabs and a lock rifle 4 slabs, sold only to those allowed to carry them. Anyone else pays double on the black market, and risks the questions. The Vartan II and SPEAR's weapons aren't sold: one off the black market costs a shell. The palm lock is never sold legally (2 slabs on the black market), and a scattergun goes for 3 slabs there. Blasting charges are sold only with a House work permit, 2 handfuls each, or double without one.

The firearms and weapons below join the weapon table, and duskwarding armor the armor table, while Duskworld is selected.

  • Lock pistol: the most common gun in Mendris, also called a revolver. It fires as fast as its rotating charged wheel can be locked after each shot.
  • Marason: a heavy pistol that blurs the line between a piercing shot and an explosion.
  • Lock rifle: the SR30 is the staple rifle of the IEE's constables, protecting justice from a good distance.
  • Vartan II: the staple rifle of SPEAR agents, made to resist the Dusk and blast it back.
  • Palm lock: a one-shot runetech pistol small enough to hide in a sleeve, made for people who aren't allowed to carry anything bigger.
  • Scattergun: a gang weapon from the Fork's workshops, a lock mechanism behind two short barrels that throw a spray of metal.
  • Blasting charge: a cartridge of kren-factory blasting powder, used by salvagers and miners, and by anyone who wants a vault open.
  • Switchaxe and chainwhip: SPEAR weapons, carried by the Dawntouched.
  • Duskwarding armor: every SPEAR agent wears a duskwarding vest; full suits are kept for the most dangerous assignments.
  • SPEAR's kit: besides their weapons and vest, agents carry flashsticks and a DLF conductor, and dawnlamps for assignments that must keep the Dusk off something (see Duskworld Devices).

Dawnlight and Dimlight

Only the Allmother's light holds back the Dusk (see The Dusk).

  • Dawnlight is created and given by the Allmother. House Callarain is gifted it through its faith and shares it with Mendris through the Beacons. It's the most powerful shield against the Dusk: it never fails. It holds back only the Dusk and its creations: it has no hold on krens or krenblooded creatures.
  • Dimlight is artificial Dawnlight, made with runetech. It does the same work to a lesser degree, and less reliably, but it's far easier to come by.
LampFunction
DawnlampCarries Dawnlight. It burns one dose an hour, and within 6 meters the exposure level is None. It needs no glint. Doses come from House Callarain: SPEAR issues them for assignments, and anyone else needs a favor with the House (a dose is worth about a slab) or the black market (2 slabs a dose, if one can be found at all).

The dimlamp, which carries Dimlight, is a glint-powered Device: see Duskworld Devices.
