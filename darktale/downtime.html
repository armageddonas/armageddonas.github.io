Downtime

Downtime

Downtime is a longer stretch of free time between adventures: weeks spent in a settlement or another safe place, with nothing pressing. It works like a rest on a larger scale: every week brings some recovery on its own, and each character spends it on a few chosen downtime actions.

Weeks of downtime

Downtime is counted in weeks. The GM decides how many weeks the party has before the next adventure starts, or before something interrupts them. A week of downtime includes its nights' rests; don't run them separately.

Each week, every character takes two downtime actions, then the week ends.

  • Repeating actions: you can take the same action more than once in a week, but each character can receive Treat only once per week.
  • Teamwork works on downtime rolls as usual.
  • No Stress: downtime rolls span days of steady work, not a burst of effort. You can't push yourself on them or pay any other Stress cost on them, including talents and racial traits.
  • Complications in downtime can follow the characters into the next adventure: a rival, a debt, a rumor, an unhappy client.

Lifestyle

Each week, every character chooses how they live and pays its upkeep. A character with a home in the settlement (their community lives there, or a patron houses them) lives Modestly for free, and pays only the difference to live better.

LifestyleUpkeep per weekEffect
RoughFreeA stable loft, a doorway, a friend's floor. The week clears only 1 Stress, and refills only 1 charge on each kit and Device.
Modest1 handfulA boarding house or a cheap inn. The week ends as normal.
Comfortable3 handfulsA good inn or rented rooms, with quiet and space to work. +1d to Project rolls. If it's your last week of downtime, you start the next adventure Determined.

You can also pay 1 more bag for one more extra action that week, but no more: money can hire help, but it can't add days to the week. +1d to Cultivate and Gather information among the wealthy and powerful.

End of the week

At the end of each week of downtime, every character:

  • clears all Stress (only 1 when living Rough),
  • heals all bashing and lethal damage and 1 aggravated wound (natural healing),
  • removes all stacks of Exhausted,
  • gets everything Prepare gives, and more: consumables restocked, all armor Wear cleared, and every kit and Device refilled to full charges,
  • refreshes their once-per-day abilities.

Lost armor rating, broken gear and defects aren't fixed on their own: they need a Project (below).

Downtime actions

  • Treat: Roll Intelligence + Medicine on a patient, giving them long-term care. On a success, they heal 1 aggravated wound. On a success with Opportunity, they heal 2 aggravated wounds instead. A medical kit gives +1d as usual. You can treat yourself.
  • Recuperate: Roll Resolve + Composure as you let go of what you've been through. On a success, heal 1 Tenacity. On a success with Opportunity, heal 2 Tenacity instead. This is the only way Tenacity heals (see Tenacity and madness). It never reduces a Scar, including Dusking.
  • Project: Work on an extended action: crafting or repairing an item, research in an archive, forging papers, training an animal. Each Project action gives rolls according to the task's interval (below). All the extended action's rules apply, including crafting's attempt limit, except one: a roll with no successes doesn't cost Stress. It ends this action's rolls instead, and you can pick the work up again with a later Project action.
  • Work: Roll an Attribute + Skill that fits a trade you can ply: Crafts for selling goods, Expression for performing, Medicine for tending the sick, Streetwise for running errands no one asks about. Each success earns 1 handful. On a success with Opportunity, you also make a useful contact or hear a lead, at the GM's call.
  • Gather information: Roll Wits or Intelligence + Investigation, Academics, Streetwise or Socialize, whichever fits how you ask around. On a success, the GM answers one question about a person, place, group or threat truthfully. On a success with Opportunity, also gain an edge: +1d on your first roll that acts on what you learned.
  • Cultivate: Roll Manipulation or Presence + Socialize, Persuasion or Streetwise, spending time with a person or group: a patron, a guild, your community. On a success, gain 1 favor with them. On a success with Opportunity, gain 2 favors, or 1 favor and a lasting contact.

Favors

A favor is something a person or group owes you. Spend it, with the GM's approval, for one piece of help they can reasonably give: an introduction, a loan of gear, a door opened, a blind eye turned, a night's shelter on the run. A favor doesn't make anyone act against their own interests. Favors last until they're spent, unless the story breaks the relationship.
